# Section 39: Smoking


## 39.1 Purpose & Scope

39.1.1  The Company recognises that the Health, Safety and Welfare of Employees, Sub-Contractors and anyone else directly
affected by the Company’s operations are of prime importance.
39.1.2  The Company has therefore developed and enforces a dedicated Smoking Policy, conforming to the requirements of
the ‘Smoke-Free Regulations 2006’.

## 39.2 Policy Principles

39.2.1  The policy is applicable to all Employees, as well as sub-contractors who undertake activities on behalf of the Company,
and any visitors to or Customers on the Company’s premises.
39.2.2  This policy applies to all smoking, including electronic cigarettes.
39.2.3  Smoking is prohibited in any part of the premises or at the entrances managed, leased or owned by the company
at any time, by any person (regardless of their status) or business, including visitors or contractors except at certain
designated outside areas.

**39.2.4**
Appropriate no smoking signage conforming to current legislation is prominently displayed at every entrance to our
smoke free premises.
39.2.5  The Company provides receptacles for smokers to dispose of cigarette butts and other smoking waste, at all outside
locations, where smoking is allowed.
39.2.6  Under no circumstances are Employees permitted to take smoking breaks in addition to normal breaks.
39.2.7  Employees are not permitted to smoke in any Company Vehicle. Smoking is also not permitted in vehicles hired by
either the Company or the Employee, nor staff owned vehicles if ever used to carry members of staff, our clients or
members of the public whilst using the vehicle on a business journey.  Company vehicles will display an appropriate
no-smoking sign in each compartment of the vehicle in which people can be carried.
39.2.8  Any Employee who does not abide by the ‘Smoking Policy and Procedure’ will be treated in accordance with the
Company’s [Disciplinary Procedure](13-disciplinary.md).

**39.2.9**
Managers are responsible for ensuring that this policy is applied and adhered to throughout their operational areas by
all staff, visitors and contractors.
