# Section 34: Paternity


## 34.1 Purpose and Scope


**34.1.1** The Company wishes to support families during the exciting but often unsettling period of childbirth.  The purpose of this Policy and Procedure is to ensure the Company’s Paternity Leave arrangements are transparent and consistently applied.

## 34.2 Contractual Benefits


**34.2.1** The Employee is entitled to the benefit of your normal terms and conditions of employment, save for terms relating to salary (unless the contract states otherwise), throughout Paternity Leave. Employees cannot be paid Statutory Paternity Pay at the same time as Statutory Sick Pay (SSP).  If the Employee is unwell before starting the period of Paternity Leave, it should be postponed.

## 34.3 Eligibility


**34.3.1** In order to qualify for Statutory Paternity Leave the following criteria must be met:
- The Employee must be an Employee of the Stovax Group for at least 26 weeks by the end of the week which falls 15 weeks before the child is born.  A MAT B1 Form, provided by the mid-wife at 24-28 weeks will provide the expected due date of the child, this is known as the date of confinement (EDOC). They must be:
- Married to, or partner of the child’s mother (i.e. in an enduring relationship) and have, or expect to have, the main responsibility (apart from the responsibility of the mother); for bringing up the child; or
- The biological father of the child, and have, or expect to have, responsibility for the upbringing of the child, in conjunction with the child’s mother;
- The partner of the mother (regardless of marital status or gender);
- Not a blood relation to the mother, namely Parent, Brother, Sister, Aunt, Uncle or Grandparent;
- Taking Paternity Leave for the sole purpose of supporting the mother, or providing care for the child.

## 34.4 Statutory Paternity Leave Entitlement and Payment


**34.4.1** Statutory Paternity Leave is granted in addition to an Employee’s normal annual leave entitlement.

**34.4.2** Statutory Paternity Leave must be taken in a block of one or two weeks.  Paternity Leave cannot, under any circumstances, be taken as individual days, or two individual weeks split over a period of time.

**34.4.3** Statutory Paternity Leave must be taken within eight weeks from the birth of the child.  Failure to do so will result in loss of entitlement.

**34.4.4** Employees may choose to start their Paternity Leave;
- On the day the child is born
- A number of days or weeks after the birth of the child, so long as it is within the 56 days period
- On the day the child is expected to be born, as detailed on the MAT B1 form provided by the mid-wife.

**34.4.5** Statutory Paternity Leave does not increase with multiple births.

**34.4.6** Statutory Paternity Pay (SPP) is set annually by the Government.

**34.4.7** If you lose the child, providing you meet all other eligible criteria, you can still take Paternity Leave if the child is: -
- Still born after 24 weeks of pregnancy
- Born alive at any point of the pregnancy

Please note that if Paternity Leave is taken in these circumstances, then the Employee does not qualify

Compassionate Leave for the same circumstances.


## 34.5 Enhanced Company Paternity Pay


**34.5.1** The Company recognises the new addition to the family is exciting, but it also places additional demands on the finances.

**34.5.2** To support Employees, where they meet the Statutory Paternity Criteria (see section 34.2) the Company has committed to increase the first week’s payment from Statutory Paternity Pay (SPP) to the equivalent of three days’ full time work. This payment will be calculated on basic pay only.  For those Employees who work in Production, Distribution, Retail or Warehouse, the payment will be calculated on three fifths of their basic working week.

**34.5.3** The Company fully supports Employees linking annual leave entitlement with Paternity Leave, to extend the period of time with the child and/or mother.  Using annual leave must be discussed and agreed in advance with your Line Manager.

## 34.6 Applying for Paternity Leave


**34.6.1** If the Employee qualifies and takes Paternity Leave, they must inform their Line Manager in writing at least 15 weeks before the beginning of the week in which the child is due to be born (ECOC).

**34.6.2** The Employee must clearly state the following:
- The relationship they have with the mother, which must meet the statutory requirements;
- The involvement they will, or are likely to have, with the child’s upbringing;
- Whether they wish to take one or two weeks Paternity Leave;
- When they wish the Paternity Leave to start.

**34.6.3** Please complete the Application for Paternity Leave Form, a copy of which is attached to this policy (Appendix 34.1)

**34.6.4** Should the Employee need to change the date the Paternity Leave starts, they will need to notify their Line Manager within 28 days.  Where possible, the Company will support floating Paternity Leave which commences following the birth of the child.

## 34.7 Abuse of the Paternity Leave Entitlement


**34.7.1** In the event the Company becomes aware of the following circumstances, the Company may consider disciplinary action.
- Where an Employee has knowingly made a fraudulent claim for Statutory Paternity Leave;
- Where an Employee has used Statutory Paternity Leave for purposes other than supporting the mother, or providing care for the child.

## 34.8 Right to Return


**34.8.1** If the Employee takes Paternity Leave for a period of 1 or 2 weeks, they are entitled to return to the same job they carried out prior to their absence.
