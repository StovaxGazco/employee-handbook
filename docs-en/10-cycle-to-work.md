# Section 10: Cycle to Work Scheme

10.

### Cycle To Work Scheme Policy And Procedure

10.1
Purpose and Scope
10.1.1
This Policy and Procedure is designed to encourage and to help those Employees who cycle to work or intend to cycle
to work, to take advantage of the tax rules currently in place to hire a bicycle at a substantial discount over the cost of
buying a bicycle outright now.  The typical savings an Employee can make vary from 25% to 39% depending on the tax
rate the Employee is subject to.
10.2
Procedure
10.2.1
The Company operates a cycle to work scheme through Cyclescheme https://www.cyclescheme.co.uk/

**10.2.2** Using the scheme administered by CycleScheme provides a wide range of shops and online stores from which the

Employee can source a bicycle.  Once the Employee has chosen a bicycle from a CycleScheme partner shop, the
Employee should go to https://www.cyclescheme.co.uk/, enter Stovax Ltd as the Company name and follow the
instructions through the process.  The Employee is guided through a simple form which they complete. The form
automatically comes through to the Company for approval.
10.2.3
Once the Company has approved and paid for the purchase, the Employee can exchange the Cyclescheme certificate
for a Cyclescheme package on line.  The Employee then contacts the retailer to arrange to pick up the equipment or
to arrange delivery.
10.2.4
The initial hire period is 12 months, in which the Employee pays a set amount from their salary each month in exchange
for the hire of the Cyclescheme package from the Company.
10.2.5
When the initial Hire Agreement ends, the Employee has several options:
- Extend the period of hire by 36 months by making a payment of 3% or 7% of the bicycle’s original value (3%
for bicycles costing less the £500 and 7% for those costing more than £500). At the end of the additional 36
months, ownership of the bicycle can be transferred to the employee at no extra cost.
- Pay to take ownership of the bicycle immediately, the cost of which HMRC currently set at 18% or 25% of the
bicycle’s original value.
- Return the bicycle to the Company.

More information about these options can be found at https://www.cyclescheme.co.uk/
10.3
Before Entering the Cycle to Work Scheme
10.3.1
Employees should note the following terms before entering the Company’s cycle scheme: -
- The scheme is intended to help Employees cycle to work, and the Employee has to confirm that this will be
the main use of the bicycle to qualify for the tax exemptions.
- If the Employee leaves before the end of the hire agreement, then the remaining hire payments will become
due for immediate payment and these will not benefit from any further tax exemptions. These will be deducted
by the Company from the Employee’s net pay.
- To comply with legislation, an employee’s salary after deducting any cycle hire payment must remain equal to
or above the national minimum wage.
10.4
Rules of the Cycle to Work Scheme

**10.4.1** The scheme is available to all Employees who have successfully completed their Probationary Period.  It is not available

to Agency Workers or other workers not employed by the Company.
10.4.2
The scheme may be closed for any reason by the Directors of the Company.
10.4.3
Each Employee who is eligible to join the Cycle to Work Scheme can choose a bicycle to a maximum value (including
VAT and allowable accessories) of £2,500.

10.4.4
The following accessories may be purchased at the same time as the bicycle and are included in the scheme.
- Safety Accessories – that help keep you and your bike safe; helmets, bike lights, reflectors and reflective
clothing, mirrors, bells, horns and locks.
- Essential Accessories – necessary to cycle commute; clothing and shoes, mudguards, panniers and racks,
backpacks and child safety seats.
- Components and Parts – that keep your bike in great shape; pumps, puncture repair kits, cycle tool kits, tyre
sealant, groupsets, wheels, crank sets and cassettes.
10.4.5
If Employees leave before the end of the hire agreement, the remaining instalments fall due immediately and do not
qualify for the tax exemption benefits and will be deducted from the Employee’s final salary.
10.4.6
If an Employee’s salary reduces (e.g. through sickness) below a level where there is insufficient salary to cover the hire
charges, or the hire charges reduce the Employee’s wage for the month below the hourly National Minimum Wage,
then the remaining instalments fall due immediately and will not benefit from the tax benefits.

**10.4.7** It is the Employee’s responsibility to insure the bicycle and the liability of risk for the bicycle is exclusively with the

Employee.  Accordingly, if the bicycle is lost, stolen, damaged and written off, the hire payments will become payable
immediately by the Employee.
10.4.8
All repair and maintenance costs of the bicycle during the rental period are to be paid by the Employee.

**10.4.9** The duty to maintain the bicycle in a safe and roadworthy condition is exclusively that of the Employee.  The Company

accepts no liability or responsibility in respect of the condition or maintenance of the bicycle and the Employee agrees
to this.

**10.4.10** At the end of the period of hire, the options as to what will be done with the bicycle will be discussed with the

Employee.  One of the options could be for the bicycle to be sold or transferred to the Employee.  See Section 10.2.5.

**10.4.11** Interpretation of these rules and circumstances not covered by the rules will be resolved by a Director of the Company.
