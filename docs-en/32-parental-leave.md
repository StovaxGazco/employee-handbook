# Section 32: Parental Leave (Statutory)


## 32.1 Purpose and Scope


**32.1.1** The Company wishes to support families. The purpose of this policy and procedure is to ensure the Company’s Parental Leave arrangements are transparent and consistently applied, in accordance with statutory rights.

## 32.2 Eligibility


**32.2.1** In order to qualify for Statutory Parental Leave the following criteria must be met:
- The Employee must be an Employee of the Stovax Group for at least 52 continuous weeks;
- The Employee must be able to demonstrate they are the parent i.e. that they have parental responsibility for the child or are registered as the child’s father, of a child under the age of eighteen, or;
- The Employee must be able to demonstrate they have adopted the child, or;
- The Employee must be able to demonstrate they are the parent of a disabled child under the age of eighteen. For the purposes of Parental Leave, a disable child is one for whom disability living allowance is awarded;
- Employees will be required to provide documentation which supports their eligibility for paternity leave, such as child’s birth certificate, adoption certificate or documentation detailing legal responsibility for the child;
- Parental Leave must be taken for the purpose of looking after the welfare of the child.

## 32.3 Statutory Parental Leave Entitlement


**32.3.1** Statutory Parental Leave is unpaid, regardless of length of service or seniority.

**32.3.2** An eligible Employee will be entitled to up to 18 weeks’ unpaid parental leave, which must be taken by the time the child is eighteen, or eighteen years from the date of adoption in adoption cases.


**32.3.3** An eligible Employee will be entitled to up to 18 weeks’ unpaid parental leave, which must be taken by the time the child is eighteen, in the case of a disabled child.

**32.3.4** Parental Leave is an individual right, so cannot be shared between parents. It applies to an individual child, not to individual employment; therefore, Employees who have already taken Parental Leave from previous Employers will have this deducted from their entitlement. References requesting confirmation on previous Parental Leave taken may be sought at the point of recruitment, or taken up at the date of the Employee’s request for Parental Leave.

**32.3.5** The right to Parental Leave applies to a single child, therefore if an Employee has twins they are entitled to 36 weeks Parental Leave.

**32.3.6** Parental Leave must be taken in one week blocks. If the Employee chooses to take their entitlement in blocks of less than one week, a full week will be deducted from their entitlement for individual days.

**32.3.7** In the case of a disabled child, Parental Leave can be taken in individual days i.e. for the use of hospital appointments.

**32.3.8** Employees can take up to a maximum of four weeks Parental Leave each calendar year.

## 32.4 Applying for Parental Leave


**32.4.1** If the Employee qualifies and wishes to take Parental Leave, the Employee must provide their Line Manager with at least 21 days’ notice of any leave they wish to take, specifying the exact dates.

**32.4.2** Employees will be required to complete the application for Parental Leave form, a copy of which is attached to this policy. The Employee will be required to attach proof of eligibility to this form. (Appendix 32.1).

**32.4.3** In exceptional circumstances, the Company reserves the right to postpone Parental Leave, where it considers the business would be unduly disrupted. Such circumstances might include a period of peak trading, several requests from Employees for the same period, or when the Employee’s absence would unduly harm the business.

**32.4.4** In such cases, the company will allow the Employee to take the equivalent time no later than six months after the original request. In such cases, the Company will give notice in writing stating the reason for the postponement and specifying alternative dates. This correspondence will be sent within seven days of the Employee’s request.


## 32.5 Abuse of the Parental Leave Entitlement


**32.5.1** In the event the Company becomes aware of the following circumstances, the Company may consider disciplinary action:
- Where an Employee has knowingly made a fraudulent claim for Statutory Parental Leave;
- Where an Employee has used Statutory Parental Leave for purposes other than supporting, or providing care for the child.

## 32.6 Right to Return


**32.6.1** If you take Parental Leave for a period of 4 weeks or less you are entitled to return to the same job you carried out prior to your absence.

**32.6.2** If you take more than 4 weeks Parental Leave in one block, you are entitled to return to the same job unless it is not reasonably practicable to permit such return.  In such a case you will be permitted to return to another job which is both suitable and appropriate in the circumstawnces.

**32.6.3** If you are a woman and take Parental Leave of less than 4 weeks immediately after your Additional [Maternity Leave](28-maternity-pregnancy.md) (see [Maternity Policy](28-maternity-pregnancy.md)), you do not have an automatic right to return to exactly the same job if it was not reasonably practicable at the end of the Parental Leave.  You are instead entitled to another job which is suitable and appropriate in all the circumstances if available.
