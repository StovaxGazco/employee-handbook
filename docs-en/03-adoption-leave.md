# Section 3: Adoption Leave


## 3.1 Purpose of the Policy


**3.1.1** The purpose of this Policy is to communicate the statutory provisions, so that Employees may understand and benefit from the protection afforded to them during Adoption Leave and after returning from Adoption Leave.

**3.1.2** The Policy encourages open communication, which will help both parties, and ensure any problems or difficulties can be resolved as quickly as possible.

## 3.2 Purpose and Scope


**3.2.1** The Company will not treat Employees seeking to adopt any less favourably as a result of their intention to exercise their right to Adoption provisions.

**3.2.2** The Company recognises it has a duty of care to the Employee during the Adoption Leave period. Employees should recognise they are still under contract and have obligations relating to professionalism and confidentiality.

**3.2.3** The Company strongly believes in, and supports, open and transparent communication during the period.

**3.2.4** If Employee adopts more than one child at the same time, they are only entitled to the Adoption leave as if they were adopting one child.

**3.2.5** Where a couple jointly adopt, only one of them will be entitled to take Adoption Leave, the other can take [Paternity Leave](34-paternity.md), assuming that the statutory criteria is met. Details on [Paternity Leave](34-paternity.md) can be found in the [Paternity Policy and Procedure](34-paternity.md) section of the Employee Handbook ([Section 34](34-paternity.md)).

**3.2.6** The adopting parents can also choose to take [Shared Parental Leave](38-shared-parental-leave.md).  The right to [Shared Parental Leave](38-shared-parental-leave.md) applies to all Employees who fulfil the relevant eligibility criteria:-
- Must have been continuously employed for at least 26 weeks before the expected date of the child being placed with them
- Must remain continuously employed until the week before any period of [Shared Parental Leave](38-shared-parental-leave.md) starts
- They have, or expect to have the main responsibility for caring for the child
- The mother has curtailed her entitlement to Adoption Leave or has returned to work
- Notice of Entitlement and Intention to take [Shared Parental Leave](38-shared-parental-leave.md) has been provided to the Employer.

(For more details see [Section 38](38-shared-parental-leave.md):  [Shared Parental Leave Policy](38-shared-parental-leave.md) & Procedure).

**3.2.7** Adoption leave is available to men and women (whether married or single) who adopt a child through an

approved agency.

**3.2.8** This Policy relates to registered and approved adoptions agencies only.  Private agreements and agencies are not covered by Statutory or Company Adoption Leave.  Employees will be required to seek specific legal advice and information in the case of private agreements and agencies.

**3.2.9** This Policy does not cover foster parents, who adopt a child they are fostering, nor step parents who adopt their partner’s child.

## 3.3 Definitions Used in the Adoption Policy and Procedure


**3.3.1** Matching Certificate: The documentation, which legally confirms the Employee’s intentions to adopt a child via a legally registered and approved agency.

**3.3.2** Qualifying Week: The 15th week before the matching process begins. This is used for the calculation of Statutory Adoption Pay.

**3.3.3** SAP:  Statutory Adoption Pay – the weekly allowance, set by the Government which if eligible is paid by the Company.

**3.3.4** OAL:  Ordinary Adoption Leave – the period of adoption leave from 1-26 weeks.

**3.3.5** AAL:  Additional Adoption Leave – the period of adoption leave from 27-52 weeks.

## 3.4 Informing Us You Wish to Take Adoption Leave


**3.4.1** The Company understands the excitement and apprehension Employees may feel during the early stages of adoption and would encourage early notification of the Employee’s intention, so that full support can be given to the Employee. Discretion will be used, as the Company recognises the Employee may wish to keep the adoption process private for a period of time.

**3.4.2** Where Employees are required to attend meetings or assessments with the Matching Agency/Social Services, they will be required to provide advance notice to the Company, utilising annual leave, where possible, for these meetings or assessments.

## 3.5 Entitlement to Adoption Leave


**3.5.1** The current entitlement of Adoption Leave is up to 52 weeks Adoption Leave. This is regardless of age, or the number of occasions Adoption Leave has been taken previously.

**3.5.2** Adoption Leave is made up of 26 weeks OAL, and 26 weeks AAL.

**3.5.3** Employees can choose to start their leave on any of the following:
- **a)** the day the child is expected to be placed with them;
- **b)** a specific number of days (up to 28 days) after the child has been placed with them;
- **c)** a pre-determined date, no more than fourteen days before the placement.

**3.5.4** The Employee must advise the Company of their chosen start date, within a maximum of seven days of being notified by the Adoption Agency that a child will be placed in their care for adoption.  If this is not possible for any reason, such advice must be given as soon as is reasonably practicable.

## 3.6 Eligibility to Adoption Pay


**3.6.1** The Employee must have:
- demonstrated they have been employed continuously for 26 weeks or more in the week they are notified
- that they have been matched with a child;
- have average earnings in the eight weeks up to and including the qualifying week, that are not less than the lower earnings limit for National Insurance Contributions;
- a ‘Matching Form’ provided by a registered Adoption Agency, showing the agreed date of the placement;
- provided appropriate notification to the Company no later than seven days after the date on which notification of the match with the child is given by the Adoption Agency.

**3.6.2** The amount of Adoption Pay provided is set by the Government each year. Payments are subject to Tax and National Insurance deductions which will vary depending on individual’s circumstances.

**3.6.3** Statutory Adoption Pay will be paid regardless of whether the Employee returns to work or not.

**3.6.4** The first six weeks are calculated at 90% of average earnings which includes overtime and any one off payments, but excluding any discretionary bonuses. The calculation period is eight weeks prior to the matching period.  However, where there has been any pay rise the Company will recalculate the payment appropriately.

## 3.8 Method of Payment


**3.8.1** In the event of any pay increase, after the calculation period, but prior to the end of the 52-week Adoption Period, the Adoption Pay will be recalculated and adjusted. In some cases, this may result in a back payment.

**3.8.2** If the Employee is not entitled to SAP a form will be provided to establish if they are eligible for Statutory Adoption Allowance. It is the Employee’s responsibility to complete and return this documentation.

## 3.9 Benefits During Adoption Leave


**3.9.1** Throughout your adoption leave you will continue to benefit from all the terms and conditions of your employment, except those relating to your salary or wages.

**3.9.2** Employees are encouraged to take any outstanding annual leave to them before the commencement of Ordinary Adoption Leave. If this is not possible, outstanding Annual Leave will be paid as a one-off payment.

**3.9.3** Employee’s/Employer’s pension contributions will continue to be made during the period when the Employee is receiving SAP, but not during any period of unpaid Additional Adoption Leave.  Please note that Employer’s pension contributions will only be made if the matching Employee’s pension contributions are made.

**3.9.4** Annual Leave will continue to be accrued during the adoption leave period.

## 3.10 Notifying Us of Your Wish to Start Adoption Leave


**3.10.1** The Company requires formal, written notification of an Employee’s intention to start adoption leave.

**3.10.2** Once the Company has received notification of the adoption leave, the Company will send a written response confirming the intention and detailing the amount of adoption leave the Employee is entitled to. (Appendix 3.1).

**3.10.3** Adoption leave can start on the following:

- **a)** the day the child is expected to be placed with you;
- **b)** a specific number of days (up to 28 days) after the child has been placed with you;
- **c)** a pre-determined date, no more than 14 days before the placement.

**3.10.4** An Employee can choose to alter their notified start date for adoption, provided 28 days’ notice in writing is given to the Company, prior to the start of the adoption leave on the revised start date.

**3.10.5** The Employee must advise the Company of their chosen start date within a maximum of 7 days of being notified by the Adoption Agency that a child will be placed in their care for adoption. If this is not possible for any reason, such advice must be given as soon as reasonably practicable.

## 3.11 Changes in Circumstances


**3.11.1** In the unfortunate event an Employee’s matching is not successful or where disruption to the placement occurs and it is terminated or where the child dies, Employees are entitled to continue receiving adoption pay, for up to eight weeks after the termination date, death or date they are notified the adoption will not be proceeding.

## 3.12 Whilst on Adoption Leave


**3.12.1** The Company recognises up to one year away from work is a significant period of time, therefore, the Company may, at the Company’s option, endeavour to maintain a reasonable level of contact with Employees.

**3.12.2** In April 2007 the Government introduced ‘Keeping in Touch Days’, also known as KITs. The Company may request that you attend the work place for up to ten days, during your Adoption Leave, without your attendance having any detrimental impact on your Adoption Pay.

**3.12.3** This attendance could be for the purpose of training, staff meetings, updates or handovers.

**3.12.4** The Company may, at its option either pay the Employee for KITs worked, or opt to operate a ‘time in lieu’ policy for the KITs.  In the event of the Company opting to operate a ‘time in lieu’ policy, Employees will be able to use their accrued time in lieu once they return to work.  This may help the Employee during the first few weeks back at work and ease

the transition from Adoption Leave to work for both Parent and child.  If the Company elects to pay KITs, the Employee
will be paid for these in the month in which the KIT day is worked.

**3.12.5** During the period of Adoption Leave, the Company may contact you about the possibility of working on KITs. You may also write to the Company to request to work on KITs.  However, you are under no obligation to agree to attend work and the Company is under no obligation to offer you KITs. It is entirely your decision and you will not be subjected to any form of detriment if you do not wish to agree.

**3.12.6** Employees will lose one week’s Statutory Adoption Pay for any week in which they agree to work for the Company over and above the 10 Keep In Touch days.

**3.12.7** The Company will endeavour to keep Employees updated on any changes in structure, appointments or vacancies in their department.

**3.12.8** The Company will use reasonable endeavours to ensure that Employees on adoption leave are invited to official Company functions.

## 3.13 Returning from Adoption Leave


**3.13.1** While the Employee is under no obligation to do so, it would assist the Company if they confirm as soon as convenient, during their Adoption Leave that they will be returning to work as expected.

**3.13.2** If the Employee decides not to return to work after Adoption Leave, they must give their notice of resignation in accordance with the terms and conditions of their Contract of Employment.

**3.13.3** The Company accepts Employee’s legal rights to return to their contractual role on the same terms and conditions as if they had not been absent.  If this return is not reasonably practical i.e. the role no longer exists, the Employee is entitled to return to a suitable role on terms which are similar to those prior to the adoption leave, or opt for redundancy.

**3.13.4** The Employee has no automatic right to return to work on a part time basis, or make changes to  their working pattern.  Should Employees wish to make changes to their working pattern or role, they  will be required to submit a formal request, as detailed in the [Flexible Working Policy and Procedure](36-flexible-working.md).

**3.13.5** The Employee must provide eight weeks’ notice, in writing, if a change in return date is required. The Company reserves the right to postpone the return to work date if the appropriate notice is not provided.

**3.13.6** The Company currently participates in a Government funded and sponsored Child Care Voucher Scheme; more information is available from Human Resources or Payroll. This scheme will only be operated as long as it is Government funded and sponsored.
