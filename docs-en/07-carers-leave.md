# Section 7: Carers Leave


## 7.1 Policy and Procedure


**7.1.1** The Company recognises and wishes to support those Employees who will sometimes require time off to provide care for someone who is not a dependant, such as a close friend or relative.

**7.1.2** Reasons why Employees may request Carer’s leave include:
- Caring for a close friend or relative who is sick;
- Taking someone they care for to a non-emergency appointment, e.g. to attend a doctor or dentist appointment.

## 7.2 Procedure


**7.2.1** In order to take Carer’s Leave, Employees must complete a request for Carers Leave Form stating why the leave is required. (See Appendix 7A).

## 7.3 Entitlement


**7.3.1** Carer’s Leave is available to all Employees.

**7.3.2** Carer’s Leave is only for genuine situations where Employees are required to provide care.  Employees should not view Carer’s Leave as additional holiday.

**7.3.3** Employees are able to take up to 5 days unpaid Carer’s Leave in a 12-month period.

**7.3.4** To quality for Carers Leave, the Employee must give notice, twice as long as the time off required. For example, if one weeks Carer Leave is required, then two weeks’ notice must be given.

**7.3.5** Carers leave can be taken in many instalments, up to 5 days, so for example, could be 10 half days.  Carer’s leave can also be taken on an hourly basis.

**7.3.5** Carers Leave will be unpaid during the period of absence from work.

## 7.4 Abuse of the Carers Leave Entitlement


**7.4.1** In the event the Company becomes aware of the following circumstances, the Company may

consider disciplinary action: -
- where an Employee has knowingly made a fraudulent claim for Carers Leave;
- where an Employee has used Carers Leave for any other reasons than those described at 7.1.2.
