# Section 23: Holiday

23.

### Holiday Policy And Procedure

23.1.
Purpose and Scope

**23.1.1** This policy is designed to ensure all Employees are aware of the company procedure.


**23.1.2** This policy applies to all permanent Employees of the company, irrespective of seniority or department.

23.2.
Policy Principles

**23.2.1** The Company’s holiday year runs from 1st January to 31st December.


**23.2.2** Holiday entitlement is as set in the Employees Personal Terms of Employment.


**23.2.3** The Employee’s contract will specify if there are any periods or dates when holiday should not be taken without the

prior permission of one of the Directors of the Company, and these terms must be followed.

**23.2.4** The Working Time Regulations 1998 provide that, for Health and Safety reasons, an Employee must use all of their

statutory holiday entitlement (28 days), which includes bank holidays, in each year unless this has been agreed in
advance by a Director of the Company. All Employee holiday allowances exceed the required statutory allowance at all
levels in the business.

**23.2.5** An Employee must not carry any unused Company holiday entitlement forward to a subsequent holiday year unless

this has been agreed in advance by a Director of the Company.
23.2.6. The Employee will not be entitled to receive pay in lieu of holiday, which is not taken, unless this has been agreed in
advance by a Director of the Company.

**23.2.7** Upon termination of the Employee’s employment the Employee will be entitled to pay in lieu of any unused basic

Company holiday entitlement.

**23.2.8** Upon termination of the Employee’s employment, the Employee will be required to repay to the Company any pay

received for holiday taken in excess of the Employee’s basic holiday entitlement up to the date of termination.  Any
sum so due may be deducted from any money owing to the Employee, including salary as defined in the Employee’s
personal Terms of Employment.
23.3.
Requesting Holiday

**23.3.1** The Company will try to co-operate with Employee’s holiday plans wherever possible, subject to the requirements of

the Company.  However, Employees should not book holidays until their request has been formally authorised by their
Line Manager.
23.3.2
Any holiday request over 10 working days needs to be approved by a Director in advance of booking the holiday.
23.3.3
Employees are encouraged to take holiday in at least week blocks rather than individual days, to ensure that they get
the full benefit of holiday rest and relaxation.

**23.3.4** Requests for annual holiday will normally be granted on a ‘first come, first served’ basis.  Owing to the needs of

the business, the Company reserves the right to limit the number of Employees in each department who may be
permitted to take holiday at any one time.  The granting of all holiday requests will be subject to adequate cover being
available and the overall needs of the Company.  Accordingly, Employees should NOT assume that all holiday requests
will be granted.

**23.3.5** Employees are required to submit holiday requests through the T&A system if they are able to access this.  If they do

not have personal access to the system, all holiday requests should be directly through the Employee’s Line Manager,
as early as possible.

**23.3.6** Employees should aim to give a minimum of two weeks’ notice for any holiday period and a minimum of four weeks’

notice for any holiday of one week or more as a minimum.

**23.3.6** Line Managers will normally endeavour to respond to holiday requests quickly, and within a maximum of 5 working

days and confirm to the Employee whether the holiday request has been granted.


**23.3.7** Requests for additional holiday beyond an Employee’s annual entitlement will not normally be granted.  In exceptional

circumstances, a Line Manager may seek a Director’s approval for such additional entitlement to be taken on an
unpaid basis.  If such a request is granted it will not set any precedent for future similar requests from that or any other
Employee.  If the Director declines such a request, then the Director’s decision shall be final.

**23.3.8** Where a Holiday Embargo period applies (as stated in individual contracts of Employment), this must be adhered to.

Any exceptions will need to be approved by a Director in advance of booking the holiday.
