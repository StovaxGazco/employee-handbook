# Section 17: Expenses


## 17.1 Policy and Procedure


**17.1.1** The Company will reimburse Employees all expenses properly incurred by the Employee in the proper performance of the Employee’s duties and approved by their Line Manager in accordance with this Policy, provided that the Employee provides the Company with supporting vouchers or other evidence of actual payment of such expenses as the Company may require.  Claims for expenditure which is over 3 months old will not be reimbursed, without the additional approval of a Director of the Company.

**17.1.2** The Company uses a Concur system for all expenses and training on this system will be provided by Finance for new users.

## 17.2 Process for Expenses


**17.2.1** Where an Employee is going to have a business need for expenses, their Line Manager must fill in an “Employee Expense Application Form”.  This can be obtained from and then returned to managementaccounts@stovax.com

**17.2.2** The Employee should be made aware of the timelines to pay all expenses.
- The Employee submits the claim
- The Manager approves the claim – 5 working days allowance
- Finance approve the claim – 5 working days allowance
- Finance process for payment – 5 working days allowance
- Payment made- Payments are made at the end of the month or the middle (normally the end of the second week), the claim will need to go through this process in time to reach one of these payment runs.  The claim needs to be processed for payment 4 days before the payment run is being sent to ensure it is picked up.

This means that it can take up to 4 weeks to pay a claim.  Any lack of detail, errors or issues with the claim being
submitted will cause delays which could result in it missing the BACS run and delaying the repayment further.

## 17.3

Company Credit Card

**17.3.1** Where a need arises for a company Credit Card, the Employee will be required to complete a “Credit Card Agreement” and either a ‘Gazco consent form, or a Stovax Consent form’.  These forms will then be sent to management accounts@stovax.com by the Employee’s Line Manager.

**17.3.2** Employees submitting claims for company cards should be aware of:
- Claims for company paid and personal paid must be submitted in a single claim.  The Employee cannot submit a personal claim and a company card claim separately.
- Items which are deemed’ out of pocket’ are personal expenses to be reimbursed.
- The claim, excluding out of pocket expenditure, must match your credit card statement sent to you each month.
- Adequate reasoning must be provided for expense in business purpose and comments section.

## 17.4 Emergency Payments


**17.4.1** In the unusual circumstances that the Employee needs to be reimbursed for an expense as an emergency, and the process requires being expedited, managementaccounts@stovax.com should be alerted.  This will need to be fully justified and approved by the Finance Director before any payment can be made.

## 17.5 Procedure for Non Mileage Claims


**17.5.1** If the Employee has a Company Credit Card and incurs personal expenses, these expenses must be included on the same claim as the company expenses.  The Employee would mark any personal expenses as ‘out of pocket’.  Claims should only be created and submitted once a month.

## 17.6 Procedure for Mileage Claims


**17.6.1** For mileage claims, the Employee should submit a mileage line and a ‘fuel for mileage’ line (a receipt should be attached to the ‘fuel for mileage line’).  The system will calculate whether there are enough receipts to claim back

the total VAT and raise an alert if not.  Receipts attached to the ‘mileage claim’ will not be picked up and will be
automatically rejected.

**17.6.2** Mileage rates applicable will be amended from time to time and a note detailing the changes circulated by the Finance Director.

**17.6.3** Where possible a pool car should be used by other drivers for short distances or a hire car for a longer journey. Where, exceptionally an Employee uses their own vehicle the Employee is responsible for ensuring that their vehicle is roadworthy and taxed and appropriately insured.  The Company’s insurance policy will cover journeys between sites or on local company business. It does not cover use of an Employee’s own car for business purposes outside the Exeter area.

## 17.7 Expense Claims


**17.7.1** Expense types to be used in the concur system are:

- Hotel – for hotel stays
- Fuel – If fuel was purchased for a pool car/ company vehicle which has no personal use according to HMRC/ company policy (such as Service Engineer vans)
- Mileage – Mileage claim when using a personal vehicle, or a company vehicle with personal use (Sales Reps). This should always be ‘out of pocket’ and fuel should not be on the Company Credit card.  This should also always be supplemented with a ‘Fuel for Mileage’ line.
- Entertainment – When taking customers/suppliers out for dinner.
- For any event/team outing that takes place involving a team/majority of a team but is not open to the whole business must be coded to ST/GZ-J100-700-400, inhouse meeting costs.
- All Expenses – For everything else.

## 17.8 Expenses


**17.8.1** Employees are entitled to reasonable meals for subsistence when travelling.  Any meals which are  excessive for the location in which they are incurred can result in a taxable benefit from HMRC’s perspective and may be rejected for reimbursement at the managers discretion.

**17.8.2** Expenses relating to other Employees may be included on a claim but must be separately identified and supported by receipts.

**17.8.3** Where several Employees are present at an event, payment should be made by the most senior Employee and the expense submitted to their Line Manager for approval.  The claim should indicate all Employees to which the expense relates. All events of this nature must be approved in advance by a Director.

**17.8.4** The details on the Expense Claim should distinguish between staff entertaining incurred with fellow Employees, and those incurred while entertaining third parties.  Full details of who is being entertained must be supplied.

**17.8.5** Reimbursement may be refused, where receipts are not provided, or submitted for further authorisation by a Director of the Company.

**17.8.6** Employees are not permitted to use Company Credit Cards for any personal expenditure without prior permission of a Director of the Company. Any accidental personal spend bust be marked as private on Concur and will result in a deduction from payroll.

**17.8.7** If the Supplier is VAT registered and the goods are subject to VAT, a VAT receipt must be submitted.  (A VAT registration number must clearly be shown).  If the Supplier is not VAT registered (e.g. small B&Bs), or the goods are not subject to VAT, a receipt must still be submitted.  However, where possible high value tooling and equipment should be purchased via a PO with an appropriate supplier.

**17.8.8** Air or Rail Travel should be economy class, unless in exceptional circumstances authorised by the Managing Director or Finance Director.

**17.8.9** The cost of hotel accommodation should be within the limits published by the Company from time to time.

Any expenditure in excess of these nightly rates must be approved by a Director prior to booking.



**17.8.10** Where an Employee stays with a friend or relative, instead of using a hotel, an allowance of £25 per night can be claimed against properly receipted expenditure.

**17.8.11** The costs of videos, films, sports club membership and Mini-Bars will not be reimbursable.

**17.8.12** Any IT equipment should not be purchased through expenses but requested through the Company’s IT Department.

**17.8.13** Any uniform required should not be purchased through expenses but requested from the Company.

**17.8.12** Expenses must be submitted through the system to the Employee’s Line Manager who will be responsible for authorising the claim and passing to the Accounts Team for payment.

## 17.9 Authorisation


**17.9.1** Any expense which constitutes a breach of policy will be required to be authorised by either the Finance Director or the Managing Director.

**17.9.2** Any expense which has been rejected more than twice will be required to be authorised by either the Finance Director or the Managing Director.

**17.9.3** Any expense which has not been completed and processed within three months of the statement date will be seen as a breach of policy and will be required to be authorised by either the Finance Director or the Managing Director.

**17.9.4** Any expenses which have not been completed and processed prior to an Employee leaving following their notice and before their final pay will be required to be authorised by either the Finance Director or the Managing Director.

## 17.10 Abuse of the Expenses Entitlement


**17.10.1** In the event the Company becomes aware of the following circumstances, the Company may consider disciplinary action: -
- Where claims are repeatedly not being submitted in accordance with the Company’s Expense Policy & Procedure,
- Where an Employee has knowingly made a fraudulent claim for expenses which were not incurred;
- Where an Employee has knowingly made a fraudulent claim for expenses which were not incurred in the course of the Company’s business.
