# Section 47: Weather — Adverse Conditions


## 47.1 Purpose & Scope


**47.1.1** The Company recognises that on occasions, weather conditions can affect whether Employees are able to travel to attend for work.

## 47.2 Policy Principles


**47.2.1** The Policy is applicable to all Employees who undertake activities on behalf of the Company.

**47.2.2** All Employees have a responsibility to make reasonable efforts to get into work at their normal place of work, or other premises if they were meant to be visiting these premises as part of their employment duties.  The Company does not require any Employee to take unnecessary or inappropriate risks in the event of adverse weather conditions in order to travel to work it is recommended that Employees should follow published travel advice from appropriately qualified public authorities.

**47.2.3** Where Employees are genuinely unable to get into work, they must notify their Line Manager in accordance with the absence regulations.  Where an Employee does not attend work for all, or part of a day, then the Employee may take this unscheduled leave as either holiday or unpaid leave, at the discretion of their Line Manager or Human Resources. The Employee should notify their Line Manager and Human Resources of their preference for holiday or unpaid leave, and their Line Manager or Human Resources will either confirm this is acceptable or explain why in exceptional circumstances, the company requires the Employee to take the absence as either holiday or unpaid leave.

**47.2.4** In the event of adverse weather, the Company will monitor the weather conditions, and for those Employees, who have come into work, may allow those who live in remote areas to leave slightly early to travel home safely. Such authorisation to leave early can only be given by either a Director of the Company.

**47.2.5** This Policy will not apply to Employees that are working away from home and get caught in adverse weather conditions whilst away from home.  In these cases, they will receive full pay for the days they are not able to get to their intended place of work.  In the event of an Employee not being able to travel to their planned destination, they should either attend work in Exeter or the above clause 47.2.3 of the policy will apply.

**47.2.6** If an Employee is able to work from home and carry out their normal duties, their Line Manager may authorise them to work from home in the event of adverse weather.

**47.2.7** Information on weather conditions, and any other urgent notices, will be updated and available for Employees at www/stovax.com/staff.
