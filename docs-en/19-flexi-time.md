# Section 19: Flexi Time


## 19.1 Purpose and Scope


**19.1.1**
The purpose of the ‘Flexi Time Policy and Procedure’ is to provide Employees with a measure of freedom over their
pattern of working hours whilst ensuring that the business needs are maintained.


**19.1.2**
The Company recognises that Employees need to balance work and other commitments and believes by introducing
these flexi time working arrangements, it will encourage both a more effective and efficient workforce, and improved
working conditions.  Flexi time working arrangements should also reduce the need for unpaid leave for appointments
or emergencies.  Appointments, where possible, should be booked outside of core hours.

## 19.2 Eligibility


**19.2.1**
This Flexi Time Scheme is a benefit for salaried staff (i.e. those that are not paid overtime).  However, there may be
some Employees who may not be eligible to participate because of business requirements.

## 19.3 Definitions


**19.3.1**
“Core Hours” are the times that all Employees must be in the office (excepting time for a lunch break).  These
are between 9am and 3pm for staff who work within the Customer Service teams, and 10am and 2pm for other
departments.

**19.3.2**
“Available Working Hours” is the range of hours when the office is open for work.  Employees are permitted to work
within these times, which are normally 7.00am until 7pm, Monday to Friday. The hours may be extended, including,
exceptionally, to a weekend, provided there is a genuine business need to work the additional hours, with the approval
of a Director.

**19.3.3**
When working remotely, Employees should clock in and out on their PCs or Lap tops to ensure accurate recording of
hours worked.  Please note that all remote working, or working outside of the normal available working hours, is only
permitted with the approval of a Director.
19.3.4 	 “Standard day” is the normal working time required per day, as per the Employee’s Terms and Conditions of
Employment.  This is usually 7.75 hours for office staff.
19.3.5  Employees who work part time will usually be eligible to participate in the Flexi Time Scheme, but arrangements will,
of necessity, be on an individual basis, depending on their contractual hours of work.

## 19.4 Procedure


**19.4.1**
Employees participating in the flexi time system must be present during “Core Hours” and must carry out their other
hours of work within the “Available Working Hours”.  Any adjustments to this should be with the prior approval of

a Director.

**19.4.2**
Managers have the discretion to require attendance at more specific times during the available working hours to meet
the needs of the business and to ensure departments are not left short staffed during normal business hours or while
other colleagues are on holiday for example.

**19.4.3**
Employees must take a daily lunch break, in line with the terms and conditions of their employment during the
working day, where over 5 hours are being worked in the day (this is usually 45 minutes).  It is not permitted for
Employees to take a shorter lunch break and accrue Flexi Time during this period.
19.4.4   It is not permitted to take lunch breaks much later in the day and then take Flexitime. So, for example you are not able
to leave at 3pm but ask your Line Manager to clock you out at 3.45pm to account for your lunch break.  However,
in most circumstances you can leave at 1.15pm and ask your Line Manager to clock you out at 2.00pm to take Flexi-
Time (should you be entitled to a 45 minute lunch break – This would further reduce if you were entitled only to a 30
minute lunch break).


## 19.5 Taking Flexi Time


**19.5.1**
Flexi Time is time worked in addition to the Employee’s normal hours of work within the “Available Working Hours”
which can then be recovered by taking the equivalent time off during the “Available Working Hours” but outside of
the “Core Hours”.  With the exception that up to one full day can be taken once per quarter, provided the relevant
department’s holiday embargo, if any, does not apply, or has been explicitly waived.

**19.5.2**
All Flexi-Time should be requested through the Time and Attendance System, and should be approved by the Line
Manager before taking.

**19.5.3**
Employees should note that they do not have the ‘right’ to come in later, or leave early, so should not assume that they
can regularly leave or come in at hours different to those in their contracts of employment.  The taking of Flexi-Time
should be with the approval of your Line Manager based on business needs and should be discussed and approved in
advance.

**19.5.4**
In exceptional circumstances, or where there is a medical appointment, Employees are able to take Flexi Time in the
core working hours, but this is only with the specific prior approval of a Director.

**19.5.5**
The taking of Flexi Time should not form part of any regular working pattern.  If a change to a working pattern on a
permanent basis is required, this should be requested through the Company’s [Right to Request Flexible Working Policy](36-flexible-working.md)
([Section 36](36-flexible-working.md) of the Employee Handbook).

**19.5.6**
Employees are permitted to go into negative equity Flexi Time as it is not always possible to plan ahead. However, no
more than 10 hours negative Flexi Time can be accrued unless in exceptional circumstance agreed with a Director.

**19.5.7**
Employees may take up to eight hours per calendar month as flexi time and one full day per quarter.

## 19.6 Carry Over

19.6.1   Employees are permitted to carry over any accrued flexi time on a rolling basis. There is no limit to the amount of
Flexi Hours that can be worked but there will be cap of 127 hours recorded on the Time and Attendance system.
(This equates to a full 12 months of available Flexi Time).  The Time and Attendance System will account for this
automatically.


## 19.7 Employee’s Responsibilities


**19.7.1**
Employees must ensure they understand the rules of the Flexi Time Scheme and :-
- Record their arrival and departure times through the Time and Attendance System;
- Must not take flexi time without the prior permission of their Line Manager;
- Must not be absent during “Core Hours”; save as permitted under section 19.5.4 and 19.5.7;
- Must accrue and take Flexi-Time in blocks of a minimum of 15 minutes;
- Must take account of a lunch break each day, in line with their contract of employment;
- Must apply for and record absences in accordance with this policy.

**19.7.2**
A breach of flexi time procedures may result in disciplinary action being taken against an Employee, as stated in the
Company’s [Disciplinary Policy](13-disciplinary.md) & Procedure as laid out in the Company’s Employee Handbook.

**19.7.3**
Employees leaving the company with hours owed to them will not receive payment for these hours upon leaving the
company.
19.7.4   Employees leaving the Company with hours owed by them agree the value of those hours will be deducted from their
final pay and explicitly authorise that deduction.


## 19.8 Line Manager Responsibilities


**19.8.1**
Line Managers have responsibility for monitoring and controlling the attendance of their Employees and the operation
of the Flexi Scheme.  In particular they must:-
- Ensure that the Employee has complied with 19.7.1 above and that the approval of a Director is sought
where relevant.
