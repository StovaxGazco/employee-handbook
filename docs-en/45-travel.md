# Section 45: Travel

45.

### Travel Policy & Procedure

45.1
Purpose of the Policy
45.1.1
This policy aims to ensure that all Employees have a clear understanding of their entitlements and requirements when
travelling on Company business.
45.1.2
This policy aims to ensure that the Company:-
- Safeguards the wellbeing, comfort, safety and security of Employees;
- Communicates our commitment to sustainability issues;
- Secure enhanced supplier service levels at the lowest possible cost.
45.1.3
This policy applies to:-
- All UK and overseas travel undertaken on Company business;
- All permanent Employees of the Company;
- All Fixed Term Employees.
45.2
UK Travel
45.2.1
Rail:  Train tickets should, as far as possible be purchased in advance of travel to ensure the best rates are obtained.
All travel by train should be booked economy or standard unless otherwise authorised by a Director of the Company.
45.2.2
Private Car:  Employees are permitted to claim motor mileage allowance.  See section 17 of the Expenses Policy &
Procedure in the Employee Handbook.
45.2.3
Company Vehicles: Employees should refer to section 17 of the Expenses Policy & Procedure in the

Employee Handbook.
45.2.4
Hire Cars:  All hire cars should be booked through the Company’s preferred car hire companies.
45.2.5
The standard of vehicle that should be booked is a group C or D.
45.2.6
There may be a few exceptions when a bigger vehicle is required, and these should be agreed in advance with

a Director.
45.2.7
Where cars are provided with a full tank of fuel, they should be returned to the car hire provider full in order to avoid
expensive refuelling costs.
45.2.8
The Employee is personally responsible for any fines incurred whilst being the registered hirer of the vehicle.
45.2.9
Taxis:  Taxis should only be used where there is no suitable public or private transport, unless the foreign travel return
policy applies.
45.3
Overseas Travel

**45.3.1** All air travel should be booked economy or standard class.  When booking air travel Employees should ensure that

they book luggage at the time of booking.
45.3.2
Employees are responsible for adding ‘Advance Passenger Information’ if required to the booking and ensuring that
they also have boarding cards ready before getting to the airport.
45.3.4
Passport & Visa:  Employees are responsible for checking the validity of their passport, noting that most countries
require a minimum validity of 6 months remaining on the passport on the date of their return. The cost and
responsibility of obtaining a new passport should be borne by the Employee.
45.3.5
In the event of a Visa being required, the Company will cover the full cost of obtaining this.
45.3.6
Returning from Abroad Overnight:  Employees should refer to Section 8.6 Company Vehicle Drivers Policy & Procedure
in the Employee handbook.

45.4
Hotel Bookings
45.4.1
Hotel bookings should be made in advance, whenever possible through booking.com.  This is a comparison site and
compares the cost of hotels through most of the main booking sites.  Bookings should be made through Company
credit cards where possible, and receipts must be kept and attached to the monthly credit card statement.
45.5
24-hour Emergency Service

**45.5.1** The Company has a 24-hour emergency travel service for worldwide medical and security  assistance when travelling

overseas.  Employees should ensure they have this information before travelling overseas on business.
45.6
Travel Insurance
45.6.1
The Company has a travel insurance policy in place. It is the Employees responsibility (and indeed they are strongly
recommended, in their own interests) to obtain a copy of the travel insurance policy and highlight any medical
condition or circumstances which may result in cover not being applicable under the policy.
45.7
Health & Safety When Travelling
45.7.1
Under the Health & Safety at Work Act 1974, all Employees must take reasonable care for their health and safety.  See
Section 22 Health & Safety Policy and Procedure in the Employee Handbook.
45.7.2
Immunisation and health information should be considered and checked for all countries.  This can be obtained f

rom www.dh.gov.uk/travellers.
45.8
Working Time Directive
45.8.1
Employees should refer to Section 50 Working Time Regulations Policy & Procedure of the Employee Handbook.
