# Section 42: Staff Purchase


## 42.1 Purpose and Scope


**42.1.1** All Employees will be entitled to participate in the Staff Purchase Scheme and will be able to buy Group products at cost + 15% after they have successfully completed their probationary period and completed the Staff Purchase

Order Form.


**42.1.2** VAT at the prevailing rate will then be added to the above price.

## 42.2 Payment Terms


**42.2.1** Any Employee purchases must be paid for in advance and those over £500 must be paid for and have funds cleared prior to collection.

**42.2.2** Any Employee purchase bought within 4 months prior to departure will be re-invoiced at normal retail price less 15%, and, if necessary, this sum will be deducted from the Employee’s wages when they leave.

**42.2.3** If payment is being made in cash it must be paid in time for it to be banked on the same day.  This will normally mean it will need to be paid to accounts by 11am.

**42.2.4** All Employee purchases must be paid for before picking up the goods. Payment must be made to the Credit Control Department for the Company concerned.

## 42.3 Eligibility


**42.3.1** Only Employees and their immediate family are eligible for staff discount, i.e.:
- Parents/Step-parents;
- Parents-in-law;
- Brother/Sister;
- Son/Daughter (Step & in-law)
- Grandparents

**42.3.2** Employees will be required to confirm for whom the Employee Purchase is being made and the address at which it will be installed or fitted when you place the order.

## 42.4 Process


**42.4.1** The attached Order Form must be completed for all orders for Stovax or Gazco products. (Appendix 42A)
