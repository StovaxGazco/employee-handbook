# Section 30: Mobile Phone


## 30.1 Policy Principles


**30.1.1** This Policy applies to mobile telephones provided by the company and also to the use of private mobile telephones while Employees are at work.

## 30.2

Company Telephones

**30.2.1** Company mobile telephones may be used for a limited number of urgent private calls or if the Employee is staying away from home on company business. Excessive levels of personal calls will be at the Employee’s expense and they will be sent an itemised bill for all personal calls.  Employees will be required to pay for such excessive personal calls either directly to the Company or the Company may deduct the sum owed from their salary.

**30.2.2** The Company monitors the use of its mobile telephones and gets fully itemised bills for all Company mobile telephones, including ‘pool’ phones.

**30.2.3** Whilst travelling overseas Employees should ensure that ‘Data Roaming’ is turned off to avoid excessive charges. Employees will be liable for excessive charges arising from Data Roaming and the Company may deduct the sum owed from their salary.

**30.2.4** The safeguarding of your company mobile telephone is your responsibility. Employees should not leave it in a visible place such as in an unattended car. The use of a personal identification number (PIN) is required to be set up on your company mobile telephone for added security.  Loss of a company mobile telephone should be reported immediately to the Facilities Manager.  Failure to report such loss will be dealt with under the disciplinary procedures.

**30.2.5** Employees must not use company mobile telephone for any unlawful purpose or to make offensive or bullying/ harassments calls/messages.  Anything falling under the heading of bullying/harassment will be dealt with under the bullying/harassment procedures.

## 30.3 Private Use During Working Hours


**30.3.1** Private mobile telephones may be used only for business purposes during working hours, although they may also be used for private purposes during working hours in the case of an emergency.

**30.3.2** Personal use of mobile telephones for reasons other than emergencies is not permitted.

## 30.4 Restrictions on Mobile Phone Use


**30.4.1** Employees should not use either company mobile telephones or private mobile telephones in a motor vehicle, unless it is fitted with a legal hands-free kit.  See Sub-[Section 8](08-company-vehicles.md).8 of [Section 8](08-company-vehicles.md), the [Company Vehicle Drivers Policy](08-company-vehicles.md) & Procedure

**30.4.2** Employees must NOT use either company mobile telephones or private mobile telephones when operating any plant or equipment either owned by the company or on company premises.

## 30.5 General use


**30.5.1** When using a mobile telephone, Employees should, at all times, have regard for other Employees working in their vicinity and keep noise levels to a volume that will not create a nuisance.

**30.5.2** All Employees using company mobile telephones should at all times be professional and courteous in their conversations and have due regard for the need to maintain the confidentiality of company data, particularly when using their phone in a public place, such as a train, restaurant or airport.
