# Section 27: Lieu Day


## 27.1 Purpose and Scope


**27.1.1** The purpose of this Policy and Procedure is to ensure the fair treatment of Employees and reflect the additional work that may need to be completed over a weekend, out of normal office hours. This policy is aimed at Employees who from time to time work additional hours, specifically weekend working in the UK and also when travelling overseas.

## 27.2 Policy Principles


**27.2.1** The Stovax Group works with other businesses that may operate outside of normal office hours and often in different time zones. To develop our expanding business, it may be a requirement for Employees to work outside of normal office hours in order to help provide the level of service and development that the Stovax Group strives to achieve.

**27.2.2** Employees who are required to work and/or travel over the weekends (i.e. both Saturday and Sunday) are usually from the Sales, Purchasing, Quality or the Product Development Teams, although this list is not exhaustive.

**27.2.3** Employees will be entitled to claim back a lieu day in respect of additional whole weekend days they are required to work or travel away from home.

**27.2.4** Choosing to have a rest day that falls on a Saturday or Sunday whilst overseas, does not constitute a working day and therefore cannot be claimed as a lieu day. The decision to confirm lieu days will be agreed by Employees’ individual Line Director.

**27.2.5** In the event of any dispute over an employee’s lieu day entitlement, then the decision of the Employee’s individual Line Director will be final. The Employee is entitled to appeal this under the Grievance procedures if they feel that the decision made is inconsistent with this policy based on the facts of their case.

## 27.3 Requesting Lieu Days


**27.3.1** Lieu days must be requested and approved by their Line Manager before they are taken. Lieu day requests must be made on the form included within this policy. (Appendix 27.1)

**27.3.2** Requests for Lieu day entitlement(s) must be made within 4 weeks of the additional time being worked for which the Lieu day is being claimed.  A request for Lieu day entitlement after the 4-week timescale must be rejected by the Employee’s Line Director.

**27.3.3** Requests to take the Lieu days as time off are subject to same rules as a request to take holiday.
