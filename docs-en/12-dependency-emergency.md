# Section 12: Dependency and Emergency Leave

12.

### Dependency And Emergency Leave Policy And Procedure

12.1.
Policy Statement

**12.1.1** The Company recognises that from time to time Employees may experience unforeseen domestic emergencies.

The purpose of this Policy and Procedure is to ensure the Company’s arrangements are transparent and applied
consistently.
12.2.
Policy Principles

**12.2.1** This policy applies to all Employees and is intended to allow time off in order to deal with the emergency only. Once

the emergency is dealt with, the Employee will be expected to return to the work place as soon as possible. In most
cases, this would normally mean returning to work no later than the following day.
12.2.2
Emergency could include, but is not limited to the following:
- Sudden and unplanned loss of childcare provisions;
- Family illness, including unplanned hospital admissions;
- Accidents or injuries that have affected a dependant;
- Household emergencies that will result in loss or damage, for example flood, fire or burglary.

**12.2.3** This policy does not apply to planned events such as domestic repairs, deliveries, refurbishments.


**12.2.4** Dependency and Emergency leave will be unpaid in most cases, unless the Managing Director or Company Secretary

agrees otherwise. A Line Manager may agree that an Employee may make up time lost due to the emergency and, in
these circumstances; the Employee will not lose any pay.
12.3.
Employee’s responsibilities

**12.3.1** If the Employee is off site, the Employee must phone the Line Manager as soon as reasonably practical, explaining the

nature of the situation and when the Employee is expected to return to work. If the Employee is on site, the Employee
must inform his Line Manager of the details of the emergency and get permission to leave under these procedures.

**12.3.2** The Company fully supports Employees linking annual leave entitlement with Dependency Leave, to extend the period

of time off, where the business allows. Using annual leave must be discussed and agreed with your Line Manager in
advance of being taken.

**12.3.3** Where the circumstances have resulted in longer term needs, Employees should refer to the ‘Right to Flexible Working

Policy & Procedure’, which can be found in the Employee Handbook.
12.3.4
A Dependency and Emergency Leave Form should be completed, signed by the Line Manager and returned to Human
Resources.  (See Appendix 12A).
12.4.
Abuse of the Dependency and Emergency Leave Entitlement
12.4.1
In the event the Company becomes aware of the following circumstances, the Company may

consider disciplinary action:
- where an Employee has knowingly made a fraudulent claim for Dependency Leave;
- where an Employee has used Dependency Leave for purposes other than supporting a family member,
dependent or dealing with an emergency.
