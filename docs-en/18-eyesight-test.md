# Section 18: Eyesight Test

18.

### Eyesight Test Policy And Procedure

18.1.
Purpose and Scope

**18.1.1** The Display Screen Equipment (DSE) Regulations 1992 (Amended 2002) relate to the correct use of display screen

equipment (also called VDU – Visual Display Unit) and place a responsibility on employers to attend to the eye care of
DSE users.

**18.1.2** The Regulations state that there is a requirement for an employer to provide eye and eyesight tests, on request, to

all current or new DSE users.  Such tests will include an examination of the health of the eye as well as its function in
accordance with the Opticians Act.
18.2.
Regularity of the Test

**18.2.1** The Employees are provided with eye and eyesight tests at regular intervals after the first test.  The professional

guidance of the optometrist should be followed when deciding when re-examination should take place.  In practice
this is usually a two-year test cycle.
18.3.
The Provider

**18.3.1** In line with the Regulations, the Company has decided to opt for one provider.  The provider the Company has chosen

is Specsavers.  Employees may, of course, choose to use a different supplier but it will be at their own cost.
18.4.
Procedure to Follow for an Eyesight Test

Step 1: Employees can obtain a pre-paid VDU Eyecare Voucher from Human Resources.

Step 2: The voucher which will be specifically for the Employee’s use.

Step 3: The voucher entitles the Employee to a full eye examination at any Specsavers Optician in the UK up to the
value of £25.00.
- **a)** A pair of £49 Pentax CR39 single vision glasses when required solely and specifically for VDU use or a £49
contribution can be used to upgrade towards other frame ranges.
- **b)** a £20 discount from the Specsavers £99 range (or above), on any spectacles purchased for general use, subject
to the Premium Club terms and conditions*.

* Premium Club discount applies to selected in-store offers only – cannot be used with over 60s discount, the designer
glasses and sunglasses at £99 offer nor the sale of Easycare™.  All terms for in-store offers apply.  Excludes contact
lenses.  One voucher per transaction.

Please note that these offers are dependent on Specsavers current terms and conditions and therefore may be
changed without notice.

Points to Note on the Use of the Eyecare Vouchers

18.6.
Safety Eyewear
