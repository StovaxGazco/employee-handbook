# Section 51: Work Related Functions & Events

51.

### Work Related Functions And Events Policy And Procedure

51.1.
Purpose and Scope

**51.1.1** The purpose of this policy is to ensure that whilst attendance at such events is enjoyable and fun for all Employees,

nonetheless, minimum standards of good behaviour must be adhered to and the organisation must comply with all its
legal obligations and responsibilities.
51.1.2
The Company recognises that Employees will be invited to work related functions and events from time to time.  Such
events may be formally organised by the Company such as the annual Christmas Party, or informally arranged by the
Employees themselves, such as Birthday celebrations for colleagues.
51.1.3
The Company encourages Employees to attend such events in order to promote socializing within the workplace and
team building.
51.2
Policy Principles
51.2.1
Work related functions and events arranged by the Company are an extension of the workplace.  The Company
recognises that alcohol will be consumed at such events but expects Employees to behave responsibly.
51.2.2
Where the Company provides alcohol at events this will be a limited amount and should not be taken as an endorsement
by the Company for Employees to drink excessively nor for any resulting improper conduct.  The Company will not be
held responsible for bad behaviour on the part of Employees nor for Employees who abuse the free alcoholic drink
quota by trading allowances.
51.2.3
Employees who fight, argue aggressively, indulge in lewd or indecent behaviour, harass other Employees, or in any way
bring the Company into disrepute, will be subject to the Company’s Disciplinary Policy up to and including dismissal.
51.2.4
Employees must not put the health, welfare and safety of other Employees at risk by their behaviour whilst under the
influence of alcohol at a work related event.  Employees whose behaviour causes, or could cause, injury to others will
be subject to the Company’s Disciplinary procedure up to and including dismissal.
51.2.5
It is illegal for Employees under the age of eighteen to consume alcohol.  Any Employees under the legal age found
drinking alcohol will be subject to disciplinary action, as will any Employee who buys or obtains alcoholic drinks for
underage Employees.
51.2.6
Employees who take illegal drugs or substances, or who are found selling or distributing illegal drugs and substances at
Company functions and events will be subject to the Company’s Disciplinary procedures, up to and including dismissal.
51.2.7
Promises made by Managers to staff whilst under the influence of alcohol will not have any contractual status.
Managers who conduct themselves in such a manner will be subject to the Company’s Disciplinary procedures, up to
and including dismissal.
51.2.8
Employees who are above the legal limit for driving must not drive vehicles owned by the Company and will be subject
to the Company’s disciplinary procedure, up to and including dismissal, if they are found to have done so. They should
not attempt to drive their own vehicles or give lifts to colleagues whilst under the influence of drink or illegal drugs or
substances.
51.2.9
Employees are expected to turn up at the workplace in a fit state to perform their contractual duties on the day after
the event if this is a normal working day.  Absences will be investigated and may result in disciplinary action.
51.3
Informal Functions and Events
51.3.1
Employees who organise informal events after work, particularly in venues close to the workplace, should recognise
that such events may be classed as ‘Work related functions’ by the courts.  It is therefore imperative that Employees
conduct themselves in a proper manner and avoid bringing the Company into disrepute.
51.3.2
Complaints about Employees conduct brought to the Company’s attention either by other Employees or by the owners
or managers of public houses and bars frequented by Employees will be fully investigated and may lead to

disciplinary action.

51.3.3
Employees must not organise celebrations or other events on the Company premises without the express permission
of a Director.
51.3.4
No alcohol is permitted at events organised on the Company’s premises.  Alcohol may only be consumed at events
organised on the Company’s premises with the express permission of a Director.
51.3.5
Employees are reminded that they should be as inclusive as possible in extending invitations to colleagues. Excluding
colleagues on the grounds of age, sex, race, disability, religion or belief, or sexual orientation may be deemed to be
discrimination.  Complaints by Employees about exclusion from after work events will be investigated and may result
in disciplinary action.
51.3.6
Promises made by managers to staff whilst under the influence of alcohol will not have any contractual status.
Managers who conduct themselves in such a manner will be subject to the Company’s Disciplinary procedures, up to
and including dismissal.
51.3.7
Employees must not put the health, welfare and safety of other Employees at risk by their behaviour while under the
influence of alcohol at a work related event.  Employees whose behaviour causes, or could cause injury to others will
be subject to the Company’s disciplinary procedure up to and including dismissal.
51.3.8
Employees who are above the legal alcohol limit for driving must not drive vehicles owned by the Company and will
be subject to Disciplinary Procedures up to and including dismissal if they are found to have done so.  They should
not attempt to drive their own vehicles or give lifts to colleagues while under the influence of drink, or illegal drugs or
substances.
51.3.9
Employees are expected to turn up at the workplace in a fit state to perform their contractual duties on the day after
the event if this is a normal working day.  Absences will be investigated and may result in disciplinary action.
