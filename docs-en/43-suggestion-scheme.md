# Section 43: Staff Suggestion Scheme


## 43.1 Purpose and Scope


**43.1.1** The Company advocates continuous improvement.  To encourage Employees to suggest ideas which will have a positive benefit on our products, services, efficiency and health & safety, we offer a cash incentive to the people who, in our opinion, make the best suggestions.

## 43.2 Policy Principles


**43.2.1** The scheme is open to all Employees of Stovax Ltd and Gazco Ltd.

## 43.3 Incentives


**43.3.1** £25 will be paid to the Employees for the best suggestion(s) that are to be implemented by Stovax Ltd and/or Gazco Ltd.

**43.3.2** £10 will be paid to Employees whose suggestions, although not implemented, deserve recognition.

## 43.4 Frequency


**43.4.1** Suggestions will be reviewed bi-monthly where possible.

**43.4.2** More than one suggestion can be made by each Employee.

## 43.5 Examples of Previously Suggested Ideas

- Faster Production Processes
- Easier installation of product
- Reduced paperwork
- Improved stock accuracy
- A reduction in Despatch errors
- A reduction in Product costs.

**43.5.1** The best ideas often come from within a team, so we would encourage all staff to think, not  only about their own work area, but also about other areas within the Company when putting forward suggestions for improvement.

## 43.6 How to make a Suggestion


**43.6.1** Submit ideas by email or by completing the form (Appendix 43A) and sending to Human Resources.

## 43.7 Conditions


**43.7.1** Judging will be carried out by Management with the Directors making the final decision and feedback given to each contributor.

**43.7.2** In the event that the same suggestion is made by different Employees, the allocation of the cash prize will be at the discretion of the Directors.

**43.7.3** A list of winners and their suggestions will be posted on Notice Boards and sent to all email users.
