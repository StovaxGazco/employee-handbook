# Section 37: Right to Request Time off to Train


## 37.1 Purpose and Scope


**37.1.1** This Policy is designed to ensure all Employees are aware of the Company procedure.

**37.1.2** This Policy applies to all permanent Employees of the Company, irrespective of seniority or department.

**37.1.3** The Stovax Group recognises the benefits of being able to support staff training and development and that affording Employees the opportunity to undertake study or training which will benefit both the Employee and the Company.

**37.1.4** These guidelines provide details of the eligibility and process relating to requests for time off for training.

## 37.2


### What is the Right to Time Off to Train?


**37.2.1** Employees have the statutory (legal) right to request time for study or training. This right is known as ‘time to train’.

## 37.3

Who Qualifies?

**37.3.1** The Employee will be eligible to make a request for ‘time to train’ if:
- **a)** The Employee is a permanent Employee and has 26 weeks’ continuous employment on the date the application is made; and
- **b)** The Employee has not made another application under the right during the last 12 months.

**37.3.2** Employees can make requests to undertake any training which they believe will improve their effectiveness in their role and the performance of the Company. The training can include accredited programmes - leading to the award of a recognised qualification - or shorter unaccredited training to help them develop specific skills relevant to their job.

**37.3.3** Employees should note that the legislation specifically relates to the right to request time off for training and there is no obligation on the Company to either pay for the time off or to pay for the training, where it agrees to such requests for time off for training.

## 37.4

The Procedure – Application Stage

**37.4.1** If the Employee meets the eligibility criteria, as detailed in section 37.3.1 they have the right to request time off

to train.

**37.4.2** The request must be dated and made in writing. The Employee must state that the application is being made under the Statutory Right to request time off to train. Appendix 37A provides a framework Request Form that Employees may wish to use when submitting a  request for Time Off to Train.

**37.4.3** The Employee is responsible for producing a carefully thought-out application. They should set out the subject matter of the proposed study or training, where and when the proposed study or training would take place, who (if anyone) would provide or supervise the study or training, what qualification (if any) the study or training would lead to; and how the Employee thinks the proposed study or training would improve their effectiveness and the performance of the Company. The application should be made well in advance, as the process may take up to 10 weeks.

**37.4.4** The onus is on the Employee to be clear and as explicit as possible. Where information is limited the Employee may be asked to expand on any points raised.

**37.4.5** Where an Employee refuses to provide the further information needed, the request will be considered withdrawn, the Employee will not then be able to make a further application for 12 months based on the same underlying facts.

## 37.5

The Meeting

**37.5.1** Where a valid request has been submitted, a meeting should be arranged between the Line Manager and Employee to discuss the request as soon as reasonably practicable. The meeting will normally take place no later than 28 days from the date on which the request was submitted, unless this cannot be arranged for reason of the Employee’s absence or the absence of the Line Manager who hears such requests, in which case the time limit will be extended by a further 28 days from the Employees or the Employee’s Line Manager’s return.



**37.5.2** The purpose of the meeting will be to discuss the request; its appropriateness to the Employee’s job and the needs of the Company; and any possible alternative arrangements that might meet the Employee’s training needs.

**37.5.3** In considering requests, the Line Manager should consider the relevance of the training; what additional contribution the Employee would be able to make to their department or the Company as a result of the training; the benefit to their current job as a result of the training; and the time required to complete the training.

**37.5.4** The Employee has the right to be accompanied by a fellow Employee at the meeting. The Employee’s chosen companion can act as a witness, ask questions, take a note of the proceedings, but may not answer questions on their behalf.  It is the Employee’s responsibility to ensure that their chosen companion is willing to act and is available to attend the meeting.

**37.5.5** If the Employee’s chosen companion is unavailable on the proposed date of the meeting, and this is advised to the Company in advance, in writing, the meeting may be postponed by up to five working days at the Employee’s request, subject to the Employee proposing an alternative time and date within five working days of the original date.

**37.5.6** Within 14 days of the date of the meeting the Line Manager must write to the Employee confirming one of the following options:
- **a)** Accepting the request and establishing a start date; or
- **b)** Confirming any compromise agreed in the meeting; or
- **c)** Rejecting the application.

**37.5.7** The Line Manager must confirm the details of the agreement in writing, including the following:
- The subject of the study or training;
- Where and when the training will take place, and over what period;
- Who will provide or supervise the training;
- What qualification (if any) the training will lead to;
- How the training time will be taken – for example how the unpaid leave will be taken, or whether the Employee will work flexibly whilst undertaking the training;
- Whether the Company is prepared to pay for, or make a contribution towards the training.

**37.5.8** Where training is agreed and funded either fully or in part by the Company, an Employee will be asked to sign a Training Agreement Form. (Appendix 37A) An Employee who leaves the company within two years of completing any externally funded training course will be liable to repay a reducing percentage of the training costs, as detailed on the Training Agreement Form.

**37.5.9** If the approval requires a change in working hours or any other contractual changes Human Resources should be contacted.

**37.5.10** The Company is not legally obliged to pay an Employee for any time off granted for study or training under the right to make a request for the time off to train.  Therefore, any time off for training agreed will normally be unpaid or subject to an agreement made for the time to be made up at an alternative time. If the leave is unpaid, the Line Manager should ensure that Human Resources are notified in order to make the necessary adjustments to salary.

**37.5.11** If the request is rejected, the Line Manager must inform the Employee in writing of:
- The basic grounds for refusing the application;
- An explanation of the business reasons for refusal (see below);
- The right of appeal.

**37.5.12** If the Employee’s application or appeal is rejected, the Company must provide a sufficient explanation as to why. The business grounds as set out in the legislation are as follows:
- The proposed study or training would not improve the Employee’s effectiveness in the business;
- The proposed study or training would not improve the performance of the business;
- The burden of additional costs;
- Agreeing to the request would have a detrimental effect on the Company’s ability meet customer demand;
- The Company would be unable to reorganise work amongst existing staff;
- The Company would be unable to recruit additional staff;
- Agreeing to the request would have a detrimental impact on quality;


- Agreeing to the request would have a detrimental impact on performance;
- There would be an insufficiency of work during the periods the Employee proposes to work;
- There are planned structural changes during the proposed study or training period.

## 37.6 Appeal


**37.6.1** If the Employee feels that they have had their application for ‘Time to Train’ unjustly rejected, they may appeal against the decision to the Company Secretary. The Employee should give written notice of their desire to appeal, setting out the grounds on which the appeal is baswed.  Notice of the Employee’s intention to appeal should be received by the Company Secretary (or another appropriate person nominated by the Company) within fourteen days of the date of letter of rejection.

**37.6.2** An appeal should be on one of the following grounds:-
- **a)** There has been a procedural irregularity, in which case details of the alleged irregularity should be given
- **b)** Further facts/information not available or considered at the time have emerged which require consideration. In these cases, full details should be given.

**37.6.3** Having reviewed the grounds of appeal the Company Secretary will, where relevant, ensure that the appeal is not as a consequence of a misunderstanding of the procedure to date.  If this appears to be the case, an initial informal discussion may be undertaken to clarify matters.  In these cases, only if this proves unsuccessful, will a formal appeal hearing be convened.

**37.6.4** The appeal will, where practicable, be heard within fourteen working days of receiving the Employee’s grounds

for appeal.

**37.6.5** An Appeal Hearing will take place at which the Employee will have the opportunity to explain their grounds of appeal.  The Employee will be informed at least two full working days in advance of the date, time and loca tion of the hearing and must take all reasonable steps to attend.  The Appeal Hearing will be conducted by the Company Secretary or a Senior Manager.  The form of the Appeal Hearing will be at the absolute discretion of the Company Secretary or Senior Manager hearing the appeal.  An appeal will usually occur on the same basis as the original meeting; in addition, it may involve a review of all the relevant documentation including the Employee’s grounds for appeal.

**37.6.6** An Employee may be accompanied to the Appeal Hearing by a fellow Employee of the Company or Trade Union Representative if they wish. If the Employee’s chosen companion is unavailable on the proposed date of the Appeal Hearing, and this is advised to the Company in advance in writing, the hearing may be postponed by up to five working days at the Employee’s request and subject to the Employee proposing an alternative time and date within five working days of the original date.

**37.6.7** Following the Appeal Hearing, the original decision reached will be reviewed. The outcome of the appeal will be relayed in writing within fourteen working days of the Appeal Hearing being held. The appeal decision will be final and cannot be further appealed.

## 37.7 Extension to Timescales


**37.7.1** There may be occasions where the Line Manager requires additional time to consider a request for Flexible Working. Any extension to the timescales must be confirmed in writing, including a full explanation for the delay.
