# Section 15: Equal Opportunities & Diversity


## 15.1 Purpose and Scope


**15.1.1** The Company is committed to providing Equal Opportunities in employment. We seek to avoid unlawful discrimination in employment, against our Employees, customers and suppliers and job applicants. We are committed to ensuring that all individuals are treated fairly and valued.

**15.1.2** The Company prides itself on an inclusive and diverse environment that centres around our core values and takes a proactive approach to diversity and inclusion. The Company is committed to creating a working environment where everyone is treated with dignity, respect and without bias. The Company wishes to build a culture that values openness, fairness and transparency; a culture where it encourages, supports and develops all Employees’ abilities to maximise their contribution to the business.

**15.1.3** Some of the Company’s core values are directly correlated to what we expect from all of our people; complete integrity, open communication, total respect, and valuing people.  These values are not just intrinsic to the way we operate as a Company but are also integral to the standards we expect from our staff too.

**15.1.4** Employees are required to assist the Company to meet its commitment to provide Equal Opportunities in employment and avoid unlawful discrimination.  Employees can be held personally liable as well as, or instead of the Company, for any act of unlawful discrimination.

**15.1.5** It is unlawful to discriminate directly or indirectly in the recruitment or employment on the grounds of:
- Sex
- Gender
- Colour
- Race/Racial Group
- Nationality, Ethnic or National Origins
- Sexual Orientation
- Religion or Belief
- Disability
- Age
- Marital Status
- Gender Reassignment
- Part Time Status

**15.1.6** Direct Discrimination is where a person is treated less favourably than another in comparable circumstances on a prohibited ground. An example of direct racial discrimination would be refusing to employ a candidate from an ethnic minority, despite them being the most suited and qualified for the role.

**15.1.7** Indirect Discrimination consists of applying a requirement or condition which, whether intentionally or not, adversely affects one group considerably more than another and which cannot be justified on commercial grounds.

**15.1.8** Acts of Discrimination, Harassment, Bullying or Victimisation against the Employees or Customers are disciplinary offences and will be dealt with under the Company’s Disciplinary Procedures. The Company has a separate Bullying, Harassment and Intimidation Policy and Procedure which details unacceptable behaviour.

**15.1.9** The Company will ensure it avoids all unlawful discrimination in all aspects of employment including:
- Recruitment and Selection
- Training and Development
- Promotional Opportunities
- Pay and Benefits

**15.1.10** The Company’s hiring process is open to all people, and success is testament to the skillset of the individual and capability and experience for the role – not their background, gender, race, religion, sexuality, etc.  This flows throughout an Employees career with the Company, with equal opportunity for all with regards to pay renumeration, evaluation and promotion opportunities, taking into account any reasonable adjustments that may be required for Candidates / Employees with a disability.



**15.1.11** Although the Company will ensure Equal Opportunities, by the use of this Policy, the Company will not promote or take any positive discrimination to provide better ratios of any potentially discriminating criteria.  Instead each and every decision will be based on the objective and commercial criteria of individual skills and abilities compared against the commercial requirements of the business.

**15.1.12** The Company respect and work in line with internationally proclaimed human rights principles and ensure that the Company does not abuse any part of such principles.  More information can be found online;

https://www.stovax.com/modern-slavery-and-human-trafficking-statement/

**15.1.13** This policy will be monitored periodically by the Company to judge its effectiveness and will be updated in accordance with changes in the law.
