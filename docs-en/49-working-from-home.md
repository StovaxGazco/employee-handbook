# Section 49: Working from Home


## 49.1 Purpose of the Policy


**49.1.1** The purpose of this policy is to give guidance to Managers and Employees on the circumstances where the Company may permit, Employees to work from home, and outlines the  management arrangements required to support this type of working arrangement.

**49.1.2** The general principle of this policy is to set out clear guidelines, which enable suitable Employees to work from home.

## 49.2 Legislation


**49.2.1** There is no specific legislation relating to working from home, however the arrangements set out in this document incorporate reference, where appropriate, to the Company’s obligations under current [Health & Safety](22-health-safety.md) legislation and the Working Time Directive.

## 49.3 Definition of Working from Home


**49.3.1** Working from home means the Employee performing specific work obligations required under their Contract of Employment from their home on an agreed basis with their Line Manager.

**49.3.2** Whether or not the Employee is permitted to work at home is entirely at the discretion of the company.  Working from home is neither a contractual nor a statutory right and the Company is under no obligation to approve any request by an Employee to work at home.

## 49.4 Requesting to Work from Home


**49.4.1** Employees wishing to work from home must secure the agreement from their Line Manager in advance.


**49.4.2** When approving requests, Line Managers need to ensure that sufficient resources are available within the team to cover the Employee’s absence from the workplace.

**49.4.3** Working from home is subject to the suitability of remote IT access. There are some defaults on home ADSL routers that may require changes to the Employee’s equipment.  This assessment/approval is subject to appropriate IT equipment being available.

## 49.5 Approving Requests for Working from Home



**49.5.1** Whilst during some exceptional times or circumstances, it may be to the company’s advantage to permit staff to work from home, we seek to have some balance on this.  Whilst we may be able to function working from home, there are some clear advantages to working more closely in the office with colleagues.

**49.5.2** There may be some exceptional circumstances where Employees are not able to work from the office, and full-time working from home will need to be agreed by your Line Director in conjunction with the HR Director.

**49.5.3** Not all Employees are able to work from home and fulfil their duties, and these Employees are expected to work from the workplace for their contracted hours.

**49.5.4** Employees, with the approval of their Line Manager, are permitted to work from home for a maximum of 2 days each week.   Employees are not permitted to save up working from home days, and work no days from home one week, and then 4 days the following week.  Each week is treated separately, with a maximum of 2 days working from home in each of those separate weeks.

**49.5.5** At all times a Line Manager can refuse an employee’s request to work from home, should this not suit business needs. All remote working needs to be agreed in advance with the Employee’s Line Manager.

**49.5.6** Line Managers should consider requests for working from home on the following criteria:-
- The nature of the Employee’s job, for instance, does the Employee’s job require regular, face-to-face contact with other Employees or members of the public, meaning that it is unsuitable for the Employee to work from home.


- The Employee’s skills, abilities and personal attributes.  The Employee’s role should be considered to determine whether the Employee can practically effectively work unsupervised at home.
- Impact to team.  The demands likely to be placed upon the Employee’s colleagues and the impact upon member of other teams with whom the Employee works with.  In other words, the Manager needs to be confident that sufficient resources are available within the team to cover the Employee’s physical absence from work.
- The suitability of the home location/home responsibilities.  The suitability of the Employee’s home location and home responsibilities should also be considered to ensure that the Employee can practically and effectively work at home.

**49.5.3** Requests for working from home, which precede or follow with medical appointments are permitted, however, the Director should ensure such requests are reasonable prior to approval. E.g. requesting to work from home for a full day after a 9am doctor’s appointment is unlikely to be appropriate, in most circumstances.   The Employee must also ensure that they clock in and out accurately so that time for the medical appointment is deducted from T&A.

**49.5.3** For Flexi-Time purposes, any hours worked at home, should be worked in accordance with the Flexi-Time Policy & Procedure.

## 49.6 Expectation of Employees who Work from Home


**49.6.1** While working at home, Employees must be engaged on agreed company work and be contactable during normal hours of business operation, subject to any hours already agreed under the Flexi-Time Policy & Procedure.

**49.6.2** Employees must notify and seek agreement from their Line Manager if for any reason during the working day they are not available.  If Employees are not available for work at any point in the day that falls outside of the Flexi-Time Policy & Procedure, this would need to be agreed with the Employee’s Line Manager, and the time taken as unpaid, special Leave.  The appropriate paperwork would need to be completed.

**49.6.3** In exceptional circumstances, [Flexi Time](19-flexi-time.md) outside of the normal Flexi-Time policy can be agreed, in conjunction with the Line Manager and HR (see [Flexi Time Policy](19-flexi-time.md) & Procedure – [Section 19](19-flexi-time.md) of the Employee Handbook).

## 49.7 IT Equipment


**49.7.1** A Company Lap-top or mobile telephone may be provided to Employees who are able to work from home.  Any request for such equipment will need to be authorised by a Company Director and agreed on a case-by-case basis.

**49.7.2** Unless it is agreed in advance with your Line Manager, and is crucial to the Employee’s performance, consumables, such as printer cartridges will not be supplied for personal printers.  Where a large amount of printing is required, the Employee should attend the workplace.

## 49.8 Telephone


**49.8.1** Unless a Company mobile telephone has been provided, the Employee will be required to use their own telephone for making occasional telephone calls whilst working at home, and the reasonable costs of these will be reimbursed in line with the expenses policy.

## 49.9 Security


**49.9.1** When working from home, the Employee must be aware of the increased risk of a security breach.  The Employee must ensure that all documentation is stored securely and that any lap top or PC is password protected, has up to date antivirus software installed and is turned off when not in use.

**49.9.2** IT equipment provided by the Company to the Employee to support working from home is for the exclusive use of that Employee alone.  The Employee is not permitted two allow family members or friends to use IT equipment provided to them.

**49.9.3** The Employee is also required to comply with the Company’s policies that cover the use of IT equipment

and applications.


## 49.10 Absence and Sickness


**49.10.1** If an Employee is unable to work on the day which they had expected to work from home, due to sickness, injury or otherwise, they must follow the Company’s [Absence Management Policy](02-absence-management.md) & Procedure.  The Employee is required to keep their Line Manager informed of a likely date of return to  work, the reason for the absence, and progress, as if they were normally attending work.

## 49.11 Disciplinary Procedures


**49.11.1** The [Disciplinary Policy](13-disciplinary.md) & Procedures apply equally to office based and working from home arrangements.  Any abuse of the working from home arrangements may be subject to disciplinary action.

## 49.12 [Health & Safety](22-health-safety.md)


**49.12.1** The Company’s [Health & Safety](22-health-safety.md) Policy remains applicable to Employees working at home.

**49.12.2** It is the responsibility of the Employee to care for their own health & safety and to make the Company aware of any issues that may have an impact on this.

**49.12.3** An Employee working from home must complete a Working At Home Risk Assessment, and ensure that their working conditions adhere to the requirements of the Risk Assessment.  Where the Employee does not have suitable working conditions, i.e. appropriate workstation, the Employee will be expected to provide this.  If the Company has specifically requested working from home, the Company will provide all the necessary equipment required.

## 49.13 Confidentiality


**49.13.1** While working from home Employees will remain subject to all confidentiality clauses contained within their Contract of Employment.  A disclosure of confidential information during the course of employment may be considered by the Company to be Gross Misconduct and grounds for termination of employment.

## 49.14 Monitoring and Review


**49.14.1** The Line Manager/Director will be responsible for monitoring the Employee’s performance while working from home.
