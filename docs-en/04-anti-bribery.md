# Section 4: Anti-Bribery


## 4.1 Purpose and Scope


**4.1.1** The Stovax Group values its reputation and is committed to maintaining the highest possible ethical standards in all its business activities. It recognises that the risks from bribery are a growing concern of corporate and public life in many countries across the world. The Stovax Group, in its ongoing efforts to keep its ethical standards and with a view to upholding its position against any form of bribery, has prepared and set out a clear policy, which should be implemented in conjunction with the Company’s [Whistle Blowing Policy and Procedure](48-whistle-blowing.md) ([Section 48](48-whistle-blowing.md)).


**4.1.2** The purpose of the policy is to set out the responsibilities of the Group’s Employees in observing and upholding the Company’s position on bribery and applies to all Stovax Group Employees worldwide, within all regions, areas and functions.

## 4.2 Policy Principles


**4.2.1** The Stovax Group prohibits the offering, the giving, the solicitation or the acceptance of any bribe, whether cash or other inducement, including without limitation:
- **a)** to or from any person or Company, whether a public official or public body, or a private person or Company, wherever situated; or
- **b)** by any individual Employee, agent or other person or Company acting on the Stovax Group’s behalf; or
- **c)** in order to gain any commercial, contractual or regulatory advantage for the Stovax Group in a way which is unethical; or
- **d)** in order to gain any personal advantage, pecuniary or otherwise, for the individual or anyone connected with the individual.

**4.2.2** For the avoidance of doubt, the Stovax Group prohibits the making of any payments to public officials for securing or accelerating routine processes and procedures (“Facilitation Payments”).

**4.2.3** To further clarify, this policy prohibits the making, giving or receiving of any inducement which results in a personal gain or advantage to the recipient or any person or body associated with the recipient, and which is intended to influence the recipient to take action which may not be solely in the best interests of the Company.

## 4.3

The Employees’ Responsibilities

**4.3.1** The prevention, detection and reporting of bribery is the responsibility of all Employees throughout the Stovax Group.

**4.3.2** If an Employee identifies any instance of bribery or probably evidence thereof, the circumstances must be reported to the Company Secretary or Managing Director.  The reporting of such an incident or suspected incident of bribery will be a ‘Protected Disclosure’ within the meaning of the Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998, if made in accordance with the Company’s [Whistle Blowing Policy and Procedure](48-whistle-blowing.md).

## 4.4 Managers Responsibilities


**4.4.1** Managers should ensure that all their Employees are aware of this policy and of their responsibilities to act in accordance with its procedures.

## 4.5 Raising Concerns and Seeking Guidance


**4.5.1** The Company’s [Whistle Blowing](48-whistle-blowing.md) policy and procedure outlined in this Employee Handbook provides details of who to contact should you have any concerns or doubts as to whether a potential act constitutes bribery.
