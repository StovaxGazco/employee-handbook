# Section 25: Key Holder


## 25.1 Purpose and Scope


**25.1.1** The purpose of this policy is to define who may be issued keys to the Company’s buildings and who may authorise the distribution of keys.

## 25.2 Definitions


**25.2.1** The Key Holder must be at least eighteen years of age.

**25.2.2** The Key Holder must have signed the document attached to show acceptance of the listed responsibilities of a Key Holder and agreement to the Key Holder Policy and Procedure.

## 25.3 Policy Principles


**25.3.1** A Director of the Company will determine the list of Employees or positions to be authorised Key Holders and may revise the list at any time.

**25.3.2** Key Holders will have the responsibility to open and close the building at the start and end of their shift.  The Key Holder must unlock, inspect the property and de-activate the security alarm. At the end of the shift, the Key Holder will do the process in reverse, activating the security system and making sure the building is secure and locked with no Employees remaining inside.

**25.3.3** Keys should not be used to access the building outside of working hours unless for specific business reasons.

**25.3.4** Keys are not to be copied.  Any new keys required will be organised by the Facilities Manager.

**25.3.5** Key Holders are responsible for protecting the keys they use at all times.  Lost keys are to be reported to the Manager/ Director immediately.

**25.3.6** The alarm fob for the security system and should not be labelled with building or address.

**25.3.7** The Facilities Manager, Facilities Assistant and Operations Director are emergency contacts for the local police and alarm companies.  In the event of an alarm activation they will be expected to attend site where possible, to de- activate the alarm and ensure that the building is locked up and safe once again.

**25.3.8** Key Holders are required to keep their Manager/Director informed of any issues that arise such as fire/potential break ins etc.
