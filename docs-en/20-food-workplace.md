# Section 20: Food in the Workplace


## 20.1 Purpose and Scope

20.1.1  Promoting healthy eating is part of promoting health in the workplace. Poor nutrition is associated with increased risk
of conditions such as heart disease, some cancers, type 2 diabetes, osteoporosis and compromised oral health. It is
also associated with obesity.

20.1.2  All Employees are advised that they should try to follow a healthy eating plan. Facilities for staff to take a tea or lunch
break on site are provided.

## 20.2 Facilities

20.2.1  The Company provides suitable areas for Employees to have their lunch and to take their breaks.  Crockery and
utensils are provided, and Employees should make use of the dishwashers on site.
20.2.2  Office Staff are actively discouraged to take their lunch breaks away from their desks or workstations. The Company is
keen to promote more mixing between staff, with all staff taking their lunch breaks in the ReFuel Lounge, or showroom
areas of the business.
20.2.3  Employees employed in the factories or the warehouses are not permitted to eat at their work stations and must take
their regular breaks in the company’s ReFuel Lounge.  Under no circumstances should food be eaten whilst in any
manufacturing or warehousing area for Health and Safety reasons. Food in these environments could be a hazard as
staff could slip or fall if any food or drink is dropped, and also the nature of manufacturing and warehousing areas do
not guarantee the cleanliness or hygiene required for an eating area.
20.2.4  To avoid spills, drinks are also not permitted unless they are in the lidded or company provided lidded mugs.
