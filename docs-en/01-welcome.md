# Section 1: Welcome

A Warm Welcome
We would like to welcome you to the Stovax Group, and in particular to Stovax Limited and Gazco Limited.
Our success to date has been built on the provision of excellent service to our customers and our future will depend on the
continuation of this philosophy.  Our ambition is to continue to grow through innovation and taking new products to market.
As a valued member of our team, you have a fantastic opportunity to join an ethical, growing business which maintains a strong
set of core values and becoming a key part of our future success.
I hope you enjoy being part of the team at Stovax and Gazco and that your efforts will help us all achieve our mission statement
which is:-
“To grow profitably, and in a sustained manner, through the provision of
Market leading excellence in the development, manufacture and distribution
of heating products from initial design to customer care”.
Alistair Compton
Group Managing Director
It’s great to have you as part of our team.
Please read this Handbook, because together with your Contract of Employment, it contains important information about what
we expect from you, and what you can expect from us.  It also sets out the Policies and Procedures that will be followed in a
variety of different circumstances.
These are organised throughout the Handbook in alphabetical order and there is an Index at the beginning to guide you.
The Human Resources Team are also always available to give guidance and help when required, so please do not hesitate to
contact us.
Caroline Carr
Group Human Resources Director
