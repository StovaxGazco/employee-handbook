# Section 16: Electric Vehicle Lease Scheme


## 16.1 Purpose and Scope


**16.1.1**
This Policy and Procedure is designed to encourage and to help Employees, to take advantage of the tax rules currently
in place to lease an Electric car at a substantial discount over the cost of buying the car outright.  The typical savings
an Employee can make vary from 25% to 60% depending on the tax rate the Employee is subject to via a Salary

Sacrifice Scheme.

## 16.2 Procedure


**16.2.1**
The Company operates a salary sacrifice Electric Car Lease Scheme through the provider ‘Love Electric’.  This scheme
provides access to all makes and models of EVs.

**16.2.2**
The salary sacrifice scheme reduces the Employee’s monthly salary by the monthly cost of the car and related services
provided under this scheme.

**16.2.3**
Employees are able to log onto the Love Electric Website https://www.loveelectric.cars/ where they can put in their
personal details regarding salary and this will show what vehicles may be available to them.

**16.2.4**
Employees then order their required vehicle through the Love Electric site.  Once the ‘order button’ is clicked, it does
not mean that the Employee is tied into the order.  The Love Electric Team will be in touch with more information
including the price, and lead times.

**16.2.5**
Once the order has been placed with the manufacturer, Love Electric will not charge a cancellation fee, but the
dealership may.  A cancellation cost could be as much as 10% of the P11D value of the vehicle.

**16.2.6**
The lease period is usually 36 months and can be up to 48 months in some cases.  Employees must choose a scheme
which fully meets their mileage expectations.

## 16.3 Before Entering the Electric Car Scheme


**16.3.1**
Employees should not be planning to retire or resign during the term of the scheme.

**16.3.2**
Built into the scheme however is an ‘Early termination protection’.  This covers a number of scenarios, as outlined in
the driver handbook, but the highlights are below:
If you change employment during the lease of the term, you can either
- **a)** Novate your contract to another Employee within the Company (if applicable)
- **b)** Transfer the contract to your new employer (if they agree)
- **c)** Pay one month’s rent as an admin fee and you will be released from the contract (unless you are resigning
within three months of taking the lease – in which case you are liable to pay up to 50% of the outstanding
lease value).

**16.3.3**
If you experience long term sick leave (longer than 4 weeks) you can pay one month’s rent as an admin fee to be
released from the contract.

**16.3.4**
In the case of [Maternity Leave](28-maternity-pregnancy.md), or Shared Parental leave, you can either continue to lease the car, as long as your salary
stays above the National Minimum Wage.  The early termination protection cover means you can keep the car rent-
free for up to 12 months during your leave period, however, please note that you will then no longer be able to make
a future ‘early termination’ claim against that particular vehicle.

## 16.4

Rules of the Electric Car Scheme.
16.4.1  Employees must be a permanent resident of the UK.

**16.4.2**
The scheme is available to all Employees who have successfully completed their Probationary Period.  It is not available
to Agency Workers or other workers not employed by the Company.

**16.4.3**
Employees will not be able to join this scheme if the salary sacrifice then takes their salary below the National

Minimum Wage.



**16.4.5**
Employees should not have more than 6 points on their Driving Licence.

**16.4.3**
The scheme may be revised or closed for any reason by the Directors of the Company.

**16.4.4**
Interpretation of these rules and circumstances not covered by the rules will be resolved by a Director of the Company.

## 16.5 Charging and Parking of Electric Vehicles


**16.5.1**
The Company premises has a number of Electric Vehicle parking spaces.  All electric vehicles can be parked and
charged in these spaces, whether they are leased through the company’s salary sacrifice scheme or not.

**16.5.2**
Employees can only charge their own vehicles.

**16.5.3**
Employees are permitted to charge their electric vehicles at work and there will be no charge for the use of

the electricity.

**16.5.4**
A contract addendum will need to be signed to enter the scheme and can be requested by emailing EVScheme@
stovax.com.

**16.5.5**
Once vehicles are fully charged, where there is a demand for electric charging points which surpasses those available,
Employees should move their vehicles to a normal parking space as soon as practically possible, so that others can
charge their vehicles. This can be monitored by the charge stand software.
16.5.6  Any employees deemed to be abusing the scheme in whatsoever way may be suspended from using the

charging points.

**16.5.7**
Electricity is not deemed as a benefit in kind by HMRC so no tax deductions will be made from pay.
