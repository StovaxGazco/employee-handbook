# Section 8: Company Vehicle Drivers


### Including Pool Cars


## 8.1 Purpose and Scope


**8.1.1** This Policy sets out the Company’s statement in relation to the safe and effective driving of Company vehicles, as well as the essential requirements in relation to the use and maintenance of such vehicles.

**8.1.2** The Company promotes safe driving. All Employees should ensure that they drive with their safety and those of other road users in mind.

**8.1.3** The Company encourages environmentally friendly road usage. Employees should carefully plan their company vehicle use to maximise the business potential with the use of the least travel distance and appropriate travel times. Satellite navigation devices are provided to assist with this, where appropriate.

**8.1.4** Employees are encouraged to ensure that the company vehicle or vehicle used for Company purposes is maintained and serviced with safety, fuel economy and general environmental protection in mind.

**8.1.5** Employees should take particular care during adverse driving conditions.

## 8.2 General



**8.2.1** The vehicle is provided for company use and, in the case of company cars, private use for normal social and domestic activities.

**8.2.2** All Employees who drive or use company vehicles are required to produce their full driving licence on the commencement of employment. The Company may require the production of the full licence at other times on giving reasonable notice and will usually do so every three years. The Employee will be expected, on request, to provide a Code so that the Company can check the Employee’s driving licence for points or disqualification. Clearly Employees are not permitted to drive company vehicles if they have been banned from driving.

**8.2.3** Employees, who are provided with a company vehicle, are required to notify the Company Immediately if they incur any form of points or penalty or licence endorsement. In the event that the licence is rescinded, they must notify the Company immediately and they must cease driving forthwith. Failure to do so will result in disciplinary action and could lead to the Company’s insurance cover being invalidated.

**8.2.4** Employees must ensure that they always drive in a safe manner.

**8.2.5** Employees must not undertake any road manoeuvres that may place others at risk.

**8.2.6** Employees must ensure their eyesight has been tested and is within the legal guidelines.

**8.2.7** Employees must plan their journey ahead and provide adequate journey time in order that they do not speed.


**8.2.8** The Company may install a tracker onto company vehicles and the information collected and monitored. The information collected may include:
- vehicle location
- speed of vehicle
- tracking of vehicle journey including time and distance
- instances of rapid deceleration and braking
- any other driving behaviour that may potentially be unsafe

**8.2.9** Any Employee who commits a road traffic offence may be subject to disciplinary action and, in serious cases, may face termination of employment.

**8.2.10** All Employees should ensure that they always take due care and attention of other road users.

**8.2.11** No Employee may drive a company vehicle where they are physically or mentally unfit to drive and Employees should immediately notify the Company of any condition that may affect the Employee’s ability to drive.


### Including Pool Cars


**8.2.12** Employees must never allow an unauthorised person to drive the vehicles, unless it is an emergency, and only then with Employee’s approval, which should preferably only be given with prior Company approval. In all circumstances, the Employee must ensure anyone driving a company vehicle has a valid driving licence.

**8.2.13** Employees will be liable for all penalties, whether for speeding, parking, congestion charge violations or any other cause. The Company reserves the right to deduct these from the Employee’s salary and may charge an additional administration fee.

## 8.3 Servicing and Maintenance


**8.3.1** Employees are responsible for ensuring that the vehicle is serviced and maintained in accordance with the required servicing schedule for the particular vehicle. Only garages authorised by the Company should be used and if the vehicle is still within warranty, only warranty enforceable parts are to be used for repairs to the vehicle. Pool vehicles will be maintained by the Facilities Department.

**8.3.2** Employees should ensure that the vehicle is roadworthy at all times and ensure that they undertake normal routine checks on the vehicle, in particular in relation to the: -
- Tyre Pressure
- Tyre Tread Depths
- Oil Levels
- Brake Fluid Levels
- Screen Wash Levels



**8.3.3** Employees should ensure that they use the correctly specified items when undertaking any top up of oils, fluids etc.

**8.3.4** Any defect that may affect road safety should be attended to at the earliest opportunity.

## 8.4 Pool/Hire Vehicles


**8.4.1** The Company encourages the use of a ‘Pool’ or ‘Hire, Vehicle as opposed to a private vehicle whilst driving on Company business. If an Employee uses their own vehicle for business, they must remember that part of their responsibilities and obligations are to ensure that:
- Employee’s Motor Insurance includes insurance for the correct class for occasional business use and geographic area to be travelled in;
- the vehicle is serviced and maintained to the Manufacturer’s recommended service schedule;
- tyre tread depths are within legal limits (the legal limit for minimum depth of the tread on your tyres is 1.6mm across the central ¾ of the tread around the complete circumference of the tyre);
- a current MOT test has been passed and a certificate issued if the vehicle is more than three years old;
- the vehicle has valid road tax

**8.4.2** Where an Employee uses company pool vehicles, they must be booked out in advance using the vehicle booking procedure. Employees are required to record mileage, refuel and, where necessary, provide details of the condition of the vehicle. All vehicle defects MUST be reported upon return.

**8.4.3** Vehicle Mileage logs must be completed before and after using a Company Vehicle. The current Mileage log requires the Employee to complete the following information:-
- Date
- Driver Name
- Mileage Start
- To and from destinations
- Start and end times
- Trip purpose
- Mileage finish.

**8.4.4** Employees are not permitted to borrow company vehicles for personal use, except in exceptional circumstances and then only when authorised by a Director.

**8.4.5** Employees should take care to ensure that the correct fuel and correct grade of fuel is used for the vehicle. A failure to do so may render Employees liable for the remedial costs to the vehicle together with potential disciplinary action.


### Including Pool Cars


**8.4.6** Company vehicles should be kept clean and tidy.

**8.4.7** If the Employee takes out a hire vehicle organised by the Company, they must ensure they have the appropriate insurance (especially if this is abroad), have a valid driving licence and conform with all other requirements of the hire company. Any damage to a hire vehicle must be reported immediately to the Facilities Manager and all hire vehicles should be returned full of fuel. Failure to do so may result in the excess fuel charge levied by the hire company being recharged to the Employee.

## 8.5 Safety Factors


**8.5.1** Employees must check the safety of the vehicle before commencement on any journey as detailed on QF-238 Vehicle Inspection Sheet.

**8.5.2** Employees must not drive whilst they may be, in any way, under the influence of alcohol or similar nor whilst they may be subject to effects from the taking of any drug or other similar substance. Failure to adhere to this may result in disciplinary action.

**8.5.3** Employees who are required to take any form of medication, which may render them unfit to drive, must notify the Company of this fact, in writing, and they should desist from driving until they receive medical confirmation that they may resume driving.

**8.5.4** Employees should not drive whilst overtired. In the event that you feel tired or drowsy you should stop at the earliest safe opportunity and take a break until you feel able to proceed. If the stop means that you will be delayed in making an appointment you should contact the person and the Company explaining the reason for the delay.

**8.5.5** Employees should always plan their journey in advance and allow time for breaks and unexpected delays.

**8.5.6** Employees should always drive safely within the restrictions of the particular road conditions.

**8.5.7** Employees should take special care during adverse weather conditions.

**8.5.8** Employees should not be distracted whilst driving and should not eat or drink whilst driving as this may place you at enhanced risk of an accident. It is better for your health and safety to take a break.

**8.5.9** Whilst on journeys the Employee should take care of their personal safety and not park the vehicle in areas where safety might be endangered. When lone driving the Employee should consider their security by internal locking of the vehicles whilst driving in areas where safety may be compromised providing this does not endanger the Employee’s general safety.

## 8.6 Returning from Abroad Overnight


**8.6.1** If the Employee is returning from an overseas trip and will be driving after midnight based on the local time zone of the location where they started their journey, or will, in any event have been traveling for over 18 hours upon final completion of their journey, the Company will either: -
- organise a taxi to the nearest railway or bus terminus and then a further taxi from their destination station to their home address. Or
- organise a taxi or driver to collect the Employee from their point of arrival back into the UK to their

home address.

The Director responsible will decide which method is used. The Company requires the Employee to take up this option
for their own safety and that of others. Employees choosing to drive themselves in these circumstances, if proven, will
be dealt with under the Company’s [Disciplinary Policy and Procedure](13-disciplinary.md).

## 8.7 Mobile/Handheld Devices


**8.7.1** It is illegal to hold and use a phone, sat nav, tablet, or any handheld device that can send or receive data, while driving. This also includes when stopped at traffic lights, queuing in traffic or using a vehicle that has a start/stop engine when not moving.


### Including Pool Cars


Employees using a vehicle that is not handsfree enabled should switch their mobile device off whilst driving and
should not make or receive any calls, send or read any text messages, or interact with the device in any way whilst they
are driving or deemed to be in control of the vehicle for the purposes of the law.

**8.7.2** Whilst handsfree device usage is deemed legal, Employees should remember that use of a mobile device, whilst driving, may be seen as a contributing factor in case of any accident investigation. Employees should ensure that safety is kept paramount in deciding to make or accept hands free calls whilst driving. The Company will fully support individuals if they decline/end calls on the grounds of safety whilst driving.

**8.7.3** If a call is received, even via handsfree, and the driving conditions are potentially difficult or hazardous the call should be terminated politely with an undertaking to return the call when you have been able to park safely and are able to do so.

**8.7.4** At no time should an Employee send or read a text or email message, take photos or videos, browse the web or play games when driving.

**8.7.5** Any contravene of section 8.7 will be dealt with seriously under the Company’s [Disciplinary Policy and Procedure](13-disciplinary.md).

## 8.8 Smoking


**8.8.1** The Company is aware of the risks that smoking has to health and the risk caused by passive smoking. The Company has adopted a general ‘No Smoking’ Policy to include electronic cigarettes.

**8.8.2** Under the Health Act 2006, and the resultant Smoking Ban effective 1st July 2008, all staff must not smoke in rental, hire or company vehicles including vans, lorries and pool vehicles.

## 8.9 Use of In-Vehicle Electronic Equipment


**8.9.1** Employees should not play any form of in-vehicle entertainment at a volume that could endanger their health or their road concentration.

**8.9.2** Employees should consider not having any distractions of this nature in the vehicle when they are driving in more difficult or hazardous conditions.

**8.9.3** The use of satellite navigation aids in vehicles should be done in a safe way and they should not distract an Employee from essential safety factors.

## 8.10 Accidents


**8.10.1** In the unfortunate event of an accident, the Employee should ensure their safety and that of any other passengers where they are able to do so.

**8.10.2** Details of the accident should be noted together with the full details of any other vehicles or persons involved

in the accident.

**8.10.3** Under no circumstances should any admission of liability be made.

**8.10.4** As soon after the accident as possible you should report the details to the Company. You should complete the Company Accident Report Form and return within 24 hours of the accident, or the next working day.

**8.10.5** If the accident has been due to any failure or neglect on the part of the Employee, then disciplinary action may be taken once the full facts of the incident have been obtained.

**8.10.6** All vehicles have breakdown cover which must be used in the event of an accident/incident. No other garage/third parties to be used without authorisation of the Facilities Manager or a Director.

## 8.11 Vehicle Usage


**8.11.1** Company Vehicles, or those insured through the Company Insurance Policy, may not be used outside of the United Kingdom, or in the country that their role is specific to, without the express written consent of the Company.


### Including Pool Cars


**8.11.2** Vehicles may only be driven by duly authorised Company Employees.

**8.11.3** All Employees driving company vehicles must possess a valid, full driving licence permitting the driving of the particular type of vehicle.

**8.11.4** All Employees will be responsible for the payment of any fines imposed as a result of the use of the vehicle or incurred whilst the vehicle is in their care and control or being driven by them. Where the vehicle they are driving is a lease vehicle, the fine will be paid by the lease company and the company will deduct the cost of the fine from the Employees’ net salary via payroll deduction. There will also be an additional administration charge per claim of £50.00 which will also be deducted via payroll deduction.

**8.11.5** Vehicles may only be used on legitimate Company business or where permitted for normal social and domestic activities.

## 8.12 Vehicle Return


**8.12.1** Employees shall return the vehicle forthwith on being requested to do so by the Company for whatever reason.

**8.12.2** The Employee shall, on termination of employment for whatever reason, return the vehicle to the appointed location on the day determined by the Company for the return of the vehicle or on the last day of employment if no other date is specified.

**8.12.3** The vehicle shall be returned in a sound, clean and tidy condition. Where this is not the case, the Company reserves the right to charge the Employee a reasonable sum towards the rectification and to deduct this sum from the Employee’s salary.

**8.12.4** The Company has a responsibility for ensuring that Employees drive in a safe and sensible manner. This Policy is subject to regular review to ensure that it remains compliant with current legislation.


### Company Driving Hours Guidelines


This Guidance must be read in conjunction with the “Company Vehicle Drivers Policy and Procedure including Pool
Vehicles” section of the Employee handbook. If you do not have a copy this is available on Public Folders or can be sent
to you on request from the HR department.

PURPOSE

To ensure that company driving is carried out in a controlled and safe manner to minimise the risk to employees to
prevent road traffic incidents.

LEGISLATION

The [Health & Safety](22-health-safety.md) at Work, etc. Act 1984. Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999.

Company Policy:

1. Restrict continuous driving to a maximum of four and a half hours before taking a break of 45 minutes.

2. Plan to stop for a 15-minute break on journeys longer than 2 and a half hours

3. Do not plan your schedule so tight that you would have to break speed limits if attempting to meet them

4. Always drive safely according to the specific road conditions at the time.

5. Do not drive or allow a person to drive without the relevant driving licence and insurance

6. Vehicles to be properly maintained as described in the Employee Handbook

7. Always wear a seat belt

8. A maximum of 9 hours driving a day should not be exceeded. Consideration should be given to the overall working day when driving forms a significant part of the day.

9. A maximum of 45 hours of driving per week should not be exceeded.

Considerations to be taken into account before driving or planning a journey:
- Make every effort not to drive between midnight and 4:00am.
- Consider whether alternatives to the journey or type of travel exist
- Give thought to work scheduling to restrict long hours of driving


### Including Pool Cars


Considerations to be taken when planning breaks:
- Any break ought to be for a minimum of 15 minutes
- The 45 min break can be broken into a 15 minute and a 30-minute break within the journey time.
- Do not park up and take your break outside the destination

RESPONSIBILITIES:

Line Manager is responsible for:
- Ensuring employees driving company vehicles on company business are insured and have a current driving license
- Ensuring that a risk assessment has been carried out for company driving
- If a risk assessment highlights an employee is a high mileage company driver, appropriate safety driving training should be considered Driver is responsible for:
- Adhering to company guidance stated above
- Driving to road traffic legislation/highway code
- The driver of a hired vehicle must adhere to the rental agreement, terms and conditions and has responsibility to ensure that the rental vehicle is roadworthy and appropriately insured
- Always wearing a seat belt
- When driving a company pool vehicle, reporting any known defect to the Facilities Manager.

Facilities Manager is responsible for:
- Ensuring any reported/ known fault is repaired
- Ensuring a pool/company vehicle is not used by an employee until any known fault has been repaired
- Keeping service/ maintenance record of pool vehicles
- Ensuring pool vehicles have a current M.O.T.
