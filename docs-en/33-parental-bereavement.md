# Section 33: Parental Bereavement


## 33.1 Purpose and Scope

33.1.1  The purpose of this Policy is to communicate the statutory provisions, and ensure all Employees are aware of the
Company procedure and the right to Parental Bereavement Leave and is an amendment to the Employment Rights
Act 1996.

**33.1.2**
Employees are reminded that the Company has a designated Employee Assistance Programme via BUPA Healthcare
which is available to all employees https://cbt.bupa.com/signup/.  This service provides counselling support to
Employees, partners and dependents of the Employee, over the age of 16 years.
33.1.3  This Policy applies to all permanent Employees of the Company, irrespective of seniority or department.

## 33.2 What is Parental Bereavement Leave?

33.2.1  Statutory Parental Bereavement Leave is a right for employed parents to be absent from work for a two-week period
following the death of a child.  All employed bereaved parents who meet the eligibility conditions, regardless of how
long they have worked for the company, will be entitled to this leave.

**33.2.2**
If more than one child dies, the Employee is entitled to 2 weeks Statutory Parental Bereavement Leave for each child.

**33.2.3**
An Employee can choose to take Parental Bereavement Leave in either 1 or 2 week’s leave.  If an Employee takes
two weeks, this can be taken in one go, or as two separate, one week leaves. For example, they could take one week
immediately after the death, and take the other week later on.

**33.2.4**
Statutory Parental Bereavement Leave can be taken in the 56 weeks following their child’s death.

**33.2.5**
This leave will apply to the:-
- Biological parent
- Adoptive parent, if the child was living with them
- Person who lived with the child and had responsibility for them for at least 4 weeks before they died
- ‘Intended parent’ – due to become the legal parent through surrogacy
- Partner of the child’s parent, if they live with the child and the child’s parent in an enduring family relationship.

## 33.3 Policy Background

33.3.1  Employed parents who lose a child from 24 weeks’ gestation or whilst already accessing an existing family related leave
and pay right, for example, Maternity or [Paternity Leave](34-paternity.md), are entitled to take/remain on that leave for its duration.

**33.3.2**
The Parental Bereavement Act would not change this entitlement, but would give employed parents an additional
right to time away from work following the death of a child under 18.

**33.3.3**
Employed parents have a ‘day one’ right to a minimum of two weeks of leave away from work, to help them to come
to terms with the loss of a child, should they wish to take it.  This means that an Employee would be eligible regardless
of their length of service.

**33.3.4**
Parental Bereavement Pay is a right for those eligible parents who meet minimum requirements relating to continuity
of employment (at least 26 weeks’ continuous employment) and earnings to be paid during that leave at the statutory
flat rate (currently £140.98 a week), or 90% of average earnings, whichever is lowest.

## 33.4 Giving Notice to take Leave


**33.4.1**
An Employee must tell their Employer (give notice) to use Statutory Parental Bereavement Leave.  To give notice,
which does not have to be in writing, the Employee must tell their Employer:
- When they want the leave to start
- Whether they want to take 1 or 2 weeks leave
- The date their child died

**33.4.2**
The regulations for giving notice depend on how long it has been since the child died.



**33.4.3**
If it is within 8 weeks (56) days of the child dying, an Employee can start their leave as soon as they give notice.

**33.4.4**
The Employee must tell their Employer before they start their leave.  This can be on the first day of leave, as long as
it is before they are due to start work.  For example, if they have started work that day, and give notice to start their
leave straight away, Statutory Parental Bereavement Leave must start the following day.

**33.4.5**
The Employee can also cancel the leave, as long as they tell the Company before their leave starts.  This can be on the
day their leave is due to start, as long as it is before they are usually due to start work.  Any cancelled leave can be
taken later by giving notice again.

**33.4.6**
If the Employee wishes to take Parental Bereavement Leave more than 8 weeks (56 days) since their child died, the
Employee must give the Company one week’s notice-
- To take the leave
- To cancel the leave
33.4.7  Any cancelled leave can be taken later by giving notice again.

**33.5.1**

## 33.6 Additional Support


**33.6.1**
The details contained within this policy are a statutory right.  However, the Company can assure each Employee that
they will work with the Employee at this most difficult time, to provide additional support and assistance where
possible.

**33.6.2**
The Company will link this policy to the Company’s [Compassionate Leave Policy](09-compassionate-leave.md) and the Company’s Sickness Policy
and Procedure, subject to the requirements of this policy.  The Company will also permit the taking of annual leave to
further extend any additional required period off work.
