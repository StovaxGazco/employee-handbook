# Section 40: Social Media

40. 1
Purpose and Scope

**40.1.1**
This policy is intended to help staff make appropriate decisions about the use of social media such as blogs, wikis, social
networking websites, podcasts, forums, message boards, or comments on web-articles, such as Twitter, Facebook,
LinkedIn and any similar media.

This policy outlines the standards that the Company require staff to observe when using social media, the circumstances
in which we will monitor the use of social media and the action we will take in respect of breaches of this policy.
40.1.2  This policy supplements the “[IT Systems Use Policy](24-it-systems.md) and Procedure” [Section 24](24-it-systems.md) of the Employee Handbook.

**40.1.3**
This policy does not form part of any contract of employment and it may be amended at any time.

**40.1.4**
All Employees are expected to comply with this policy at all times to protect the privacy, confidentiality, and interests
of the Company and its services, the Employees, customers and competitors.

**40.1.5**
Breach of this policy may be dealt with under the “[Disciplinary Policy and Procedure](13-disciplinary.md)” section 13 of the Employee
Handbook.  In serious cases, a breach of the “Social Media Policy and Procedure” may be treated as gross misconduct
leading to summary dismissal.

## 40.2 Who is covered by the policy


**40.2.1**
This policy covers all Employees working at all levels and grades, including Senior Managers, Directors, Employees,
casual and Agency Staff.

**40.2.2**
All Employees are responsible for their own compliance with this policy and for ensuring that it is consistently applied.
All Employees should ensure that they take the time to read and understand it.  Any breach of the policy should be
reported to Human Resources.  Questions relating to the content or application of this policy should be directed to
Human Resources.

**40.2.3**
Only the authorised Employees within the Marketing Department are permitted to post material on a social media
website in the Company’s name and on the Company’s behalf.  Any breach of this restriction will amount to gross
misconduct.

## 40.3 Personal Use of Social Media Sites


**40.3.1**
Employees are not permitted to use Social Media Sites during working hours, or on Company property unless in
relation to their role, and with authority to do so.

**40.3.2**
When using Social Media Sites outside of work or working hours Employees must adhere to the following

general rules: -
- Employees are not permitted to post, forward, or post a link to any abusive, obscene, discriminatory, harassing
derogatory or defamatory content about the Company or any of its Employees.
- Any Employee who feels that they have been harassed or bullied, or are offended by material posted or
uploaded by a colleague onto a social media website should inform their Line Manager or Human Resources.
- Employees should never disclose commercially sensitive, anti-competitive, private or confidential information.
If you are unsure whether the information you wish to share falls within one of these categories, this should
be discussed with your Line manager or Human Resources.
- Employees should be honest and open, but be mindful of the impact their contribution might make to
people’s perceptions of the Company.  If Employees make a mistake in a contribution, they should be prompt
in admitting and correcting it.
- Employees are personally responsible for content they publish into social media tools and should be aware
that what they publish will be public for many years.


- Employees should not escalate heated discussions with any of their colleagues, but try to be conciliatory and
respectful.  They should never contribute to a discussion if they are angry or upset, and should return to it
later when they can contribute in a calm and rational manner.
- If an Employee feels even slightly uneasy about something they are about to post or publish, then they should
not do it.  If in doubt, this should always be discussed it with their Line Manager or Human Resources.
- Employees should not discuss colleagues, competitors, customers or suppliers without their prior approval.
- Employees should always consider other’s privacy and avoid topics that may be inflammatory.
- If Employees notice any content posted on social media about the Company (whether complimentary or
critical) please report it to your Line Manager or Human Resources.
- This list is not exhaustive and would be extended to cover any actions that might reasonably be expected to
cause damage or offence to the Company, its Employees or suppliers.

## 40.4 Monitoring of Social Media Websites


**40.4.1**
Employees should be aware that any inappropriate use of social media websites (whether or not accessed for work
purposes) and as defined in section 36.3.2, if breaches of this policy are found, this may result in action being taken
under the Company’s “[Disciplinary Policy and Procedure](13-disciplinary.md)” [Section 13](13-disciplinary.md) of the Employee Handbook.

**40.4.2**
Misuse of social media websites can, in certain circumstances, constitute a criminal offence or otherwise give rise
to legal liability against the Employee and the Company.  It may also cause embarrassment to the Company and its
customers or suppliers.

In particular, uploading, forwarding or posting a link to any of the following types of material on a social media website,
whether in a professional or personal capacity, will amount to gross misconduct (this list is not exhaustive).
- Pornographic material (that is, writing, pictures, films and video clips of a sexually explicit or arousing nature);
- A false and defamatory statement about any person or organisation;
- Material which is offensive, obscene, criminal discriminatory, derogatory or may cause embarrassment to the
Company, Customers, Suppliers and Employees;
- Confidential information about the Company or any of its Employees (which you do not have express authority
to disseminate);
- Any other statement which is likely to create any liability (whether criminal or civil) and whether for you or
for the Company;
- Material in breach of copyright or other intellectual property rights, or which invades the privacy of any
person.

**40.4.3**
Any such action will be addressed under the “[Disciplinary Policy and Procedure](13-disciplinary.md)” [Section 13](13-disciplinary.md) of the Employee Handbook.


**40.4.4**
If Employees notice any use of social media by other Employees in breach of this policy this should be reported to your
Line Manager or Human Resources.
